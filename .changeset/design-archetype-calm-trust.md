Design archetypes: complete design languages an app is built from, starting with **Calm trust**
(`calm-trust`: cool slate, one deep blue, quiet surfaces and tabular figures, for money, health and
anything where people need to feel safe). It's a preset extended from `minimal`, passing every
contrast pair in both modes. `archetypes` (exported, and a new `archetypes` section in
`catalog.json`) adds what an app needs beyond the tokens: a discovery card (who it's for, a pitch,
resolved swatches), type, icons, a chart palette, density, six layout recipes as framework-free HTML
(`@mariachi/design/archetypes/<id>/recipes/<name>.html`) and a `DESIGN.md` for agents
(`@mariachi/design/archetypes/<id>/DESIGN.md`).
