# Show progress

**The job:** show how far along something is: a budget this month, a set-up, a load.

## What Cirrus does

- **Budgets:** 6px round bars on a pale track, green while on track, amber past 85%, red past 100%; the spent and budget figures at 550 either side.
- **The month's pace:** a 2.5px line that warms from red through amber to green over a dashed grey line for the plan, a small green flag with the figure ("$212 below pace") and a white dot with a green ring at today.
- **A set-up flow:** "Step 2 of 3" in muted text over a thin blue bar.
- **Finishing set-up** in the sidebar: an amber row with what's left ("2 left").
- **Loading:** pale skeleton bars at the size of the text and figures that are coming; a 2px spinner only inside a button.

## Where it appears

- `dashboard` (pace, budgets), `settings` (budgets table, the month's ring), `onboarding` (steps). Source: observed on the dashboard and categories screenshots and the site's product pictures (private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- **A ring** when the whole month is one figure (settings); bars when categories are compared.

## What it does not do

- No percentage text on bars; the figures say it.
- No spinner over a page or a panel.
- No blue budget bars; blue is for set-up progress only.

## Why

- `inferred`: a budget's colour is the answer people come for, so the bar's colour carries it and the numbers confirm it.
