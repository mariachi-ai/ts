# Commit to the main action

**The job:** make the one thing a screen is for easy to find and do.

## What Cirrus does

- **One blue button per view,** solid with white text at 620, 8px corners; everything else is a white secondary with a hairline or a ghost.
- **In a card or a flow,** the action is full width, 44px, with 12px corners, at the foot (onboarding's Continue, sign-in's Continue with email); a ghost Back under it.
- **On the sign-in card,** the alternative way in is the same size in ink, under the blue one.
- **For a selection,** a floating white bar with 12px corners and the soft lift, centred at the foot of the list: × to clear, "3 selected", a blue Review, then secondary Category and Tag and a more button.
- **On public pages,** a blue pill: in the header ("Try it free"), and full width at the foot of the pricing card.

## Where it appears

- `onboarding`, `sign-in`, `list-detail` (the floating bar), `errors` (Save transaction), `empty-state`, `pricing`, `reading` (the header's pill).
- Source: observed on the sign-in card (two 400 × 44 buttons, 12px, 15/620, measured), the site's header pill (one primary in the first window of every measured page) and the app's floating bar (screenshot) (private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- **A page that only shows** (dashboard, settings) has no blue button.
- **Destructive:** a solid red button, only inside the confirmation dialog.

## What it does not do

- No two blue buttons side by side.
- No blue outline buttons.
- No icon-only primary.

## Why

- `inferred`: with one blue on a calm page, the next step is never a search.
