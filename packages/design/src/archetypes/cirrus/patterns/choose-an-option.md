# Choose an option

**The job:** let someone pick one of a few options: a period, a range, a plan, a starting point.

## What Cirrus does

- **Segmented control:** a pale track, fully round, 4px inside; the chosen segment white with the small shadow; labels at 550. Large on public pages (44px segments at 20px: Yearly / Monthly).
- **Range pills** under a chart (1W 1M 3M YTD 1Y ALL): no track; the current one on the quiet fill.
- **Choice cards** in a flow: bordered cards with 12px corners, a radio, a title at 550 and one muted line; the chosen card pale blue with a blue edge.
- **Menus** for one value in a row (a category): white, 12px corners, 40px rows with emoji, the highlighted row pale blue with blue text.

## Where it appears

- `pricing` (period), `dashboard` (range), `onboarding` (starting point), `list-detail` (the category pill opens a menu), `settings` (a small select).
- Source: observed on the pricing section, the dashboard screenshots and the app's menus (private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- Two or three short labels → segmented control. A chart's period → range pills. Options that need a sentence → choice cards. Many options → a menu.

## What it does not do

- No radio buttons in a bare list.
- No dropdown for two options.
- No blue fill on a chosen segment; the segment turns white.

## Why

- `inferred`: the choice should look like part of the surface it changes, so it stays quiet (white on a pale track) and the content carries the colour.
