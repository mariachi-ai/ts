# Never

What Cirrus refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source screens (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never two blue buttons in one view.** `observed`: one primary action in the first window of every measured source page (`emphasis.primaries` 1 [0, 1.1]): the site header's pill, the sign-in card's Continue. The app screenshots show one blue button per screen at most (the floating bar's Review).
2. **Never black text, never a grey page with grey panels.** Ink is navy; the page is a cool off-white with white panels. `observed`: the app's theme values and every studied screen (private notes).
3. **Never a heavy shadow.** Panels sit on a hairline with a 2% shadow; only menus, toasts and the floating bar lift (10%). `observed`: the app's stylesheet values; the site's cards draw their edge as a 0.5px inset hairline.
4. **Never a category as plain text or an outlined pill.** Always the tinted capital pill led by its emoji. `observed`: every category on the app screenshots.
5. **Never a full-size currency sign.** `observed`: every amount on the app screenshots and the site's product pictures.
6. **Never red for an ordinary expense.** Red is over budget, an error or a red account dot. Money out is navy. `observed`: the activity and budgets screenshots.
7. **Never a ruled grid for activity.** Rows are grouped by day with space; the only rules are under the header bands and between settings rows. `observed`: the activity screenshot.
8. **Never a fourth weight in the app**, except the sign-in card's bold "create an account" link. 450, 550 and 620. `observed`: the sign-in card uses 450/550/620/700, with 700 only on that link; the site uses 430/500/550/600.
9. **Never a page without one big line.** Every app page's first window carries a 28px figure or title; public pages a 48px title. `observed`: the source's largest text is 1.75–3 times the body on every measured kind (`type.headingRatio` [1.75, 3]).
10. **Never a spinner over a page or a panel.** Skeleton bars at the size of what is coming; spinners only inside buttons. `inferred`: no page-level spinner on any studied screen.
11. **Never a footer in the app, never a sidebar on a public page.** `observed`: the sign-in card and the app's 404 have neither; every site page ends with the footer.
