# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **What do the signed-in screens measure?** Every app recipe follows public screenshots of the app; the only app screens that could be measured are the sign-in card and the 404. Row heights, text sizes and the header's title size in the app are read from the language's own tokens, not measured. Measurements of the real dashboard, activity and settings would confirm or correct them.
2. **Is the page's big line a figure or a title?** The language puts a 28px figure or title in every app page's first window (the sign-in card's title is 28/550). On the app screenshots the page title in the header is small and the big text is a figure. Does every app screen have one, or do some pages (settings) have no big line at all?
3. **Buttons at 620 or 550?** The sign-in card's buttons are 620; the site's pills are 550. The library sets app buttons at 620 and public pills at 550. Do the app's in-page buttons (Review, Mark as reviewed) follow the sign-in card?
4. **Does a 404 inside the app get a blue button?** The app's own 404 has only a quiet text link home; the site's 404 has a blue pill. The errors recipe gives its whole-page 404 a blue "Back to overview". Which is intended?
5. **Where does settings live?** No settings screen is public; the recipe uses the categories-and-budgets screen plus switch rows. Is settings a page with its own sections, a dialog, or part of each area?
6. **How should a phone show the areas?** The phone app opens on the page with the areas as scrolling tabs in a blue header; the recipes put the page first and drop the sidebar under it. Should the library gain a phone-only tab row?
7. **How many category hues?** The source gives each category its own hue from about a dozen; the library has five tones plus the chart colours. Should pills take the chart colours as well?
8. **Mono labels inside the app?** Mono capitals appear on public pages (posts, pricing, the site's 404 card) and not on the app screenshots. Is that a public-page voice only?
9. **Empty states:** none are public. Is the pale-blue tile with one sentence and a "find it for me" action how the app says nothing is here?
10. **Dark mode:** the dark tokens are the web app's; the older desktop app used solid category pills in dark. Should dark pills stay tinted?
