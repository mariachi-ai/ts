# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **How many lime fills does an app screen carry?** The library keeps one lime pill per window. One public screenshot of the signed-in app's balance page shows its five money actions as lime circles with labels under them, and its Filters and Download as lime pills. Is that the current app, and is lime there the colour of every money action rather than of one?
2. **What do the signed-in screens measure?** The app is behind a login; its layout follows public screenshots, and its row heights, text sizes and widths are the language's own tokens, not measurements. Measurements of the real home, payments and settings would confirm or correct them.
3. **Is the dashboard's big figure 40px?** Public screenshots show the balance in the text face at 600, much larger than the section titles; 40px is the language's figure size, not a measured one.
4. **Where does a payment's detail open?** The library opens it in a side panel beside the list. Is it a panel over the list, a page of its own, or a panel beside it?
5. **Does settings ever lead with an action?** The library gives settings no lime pill; the screenshot of a settings-like page has none. Is that true of every settings page?
6. **Is a destructive action a solid red pill?** The library uses one to close an account; the source's public pages draw "cancel" and "remove" as plain text. Which does the app use for a destructive confirm?
7. **What weight is the product's name?** The source's name is a drawn logo; the language draws it in the display face at 600 so screens keep two text weights. Would the team rather it read heavier?
8. **How does the side column behave on a phone?** The library puts the page first and drops the column below it; the phone app has a bottom tab bar instead. Should the library gain a phone tab bar?
9. **The help pages' text colour:** articles set their running text in the secondary grey and their titles in near-black. Does the app's long text (descriptions, terms) follow the help pages?
10. **Dark mode:** the dark tokens come from the source's published dark theme; no dark screen of the app was seen. Does the app have a dark mode in use?
