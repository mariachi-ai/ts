# Never

What Fennel refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source's screens (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never a second lime pill in a window, never lime on large areas inside the product.** `observed`: the source's public pages show 1 [0, 2] primary actions in a window; lime fills only the primary pill in its published component styles. The language has one per window (none on settings). One public screenshot of a balance page shows its money actions as lime circles; that conflict is open in [questions.md](./questions.md).
2. **Never white text on lime, or lime text on white.** `observed`: the source's primary pill is lime with forest-green text in its published tokens; forest green with lime text is its inverse.
3. **Never a third text weight on a product or help page.** 400 and 600 only; the display face's 900 is for marketing headlines. `observed`: 2 weights on every studied help and not-found page (`type.weights` typically 2 on the list, detail and error kinds); 3 to 4 on marketing pages, and up to 3 on the public money tools.
4. **Never the display face inside the product.** Not for titles, labels or figures; money figures are the text face at 600. `observed`: public screenshots of the signed-in app set balances and titles in the text face; the help pages' titles are 30px/600.
5. **Never a border or a shadow on a card that sits on the page.** Cards are the neutral fill; only overlays (menus, tooltips, the side panel) take the soft halo. `observed`: the source's card and table styles (private notes).
6. **Never a square button, chip or nav item, and never an icon without its circle in a row.** `observed`: the source's button, chip and list-item styles; the signed-in app's rows on public screenshots.
7. **Never red or a minus sign for money going out.** Outgoing is plain text; incoming takes a plus and green. `observed`: "+ 20 EUR" in green on a public screenshot of the app; the source's notes.
8. **Never a fill on the current nav item, never an offer card in the side column.** The current item is forest-green text at 600. `observed`: public screenshots of the signed-in app's side column.
9. **Never a footer in the app, never a side column on a public page.** `observed`: every studied public page ends with the footer on the neutral fill (18px/600 headings, forest links); the app's screenshots have none. `inferred` for the flows.
10. **Never a table for activity.** Rows led by circles; tables only for real columns of figures. `inferred` from the app's screenshots, where payments and settings are rows.
