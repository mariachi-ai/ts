# Commit to the main action

**The job:** make the one thing to do next unmistakable.

## What Fennel does

- **One bright lime pill** with forest-green text at 600: "Send" on the dashboard, "Continue" and
  "Log in" at the end of a flow, "Add a recipient" on an empty list, "Register" in the site header.
- Pills come in three heights: 32px in bars and rows, 40px by default, 48px at the end of a form,
  where it is full width.
- Every other action is a pale-lime pill with forest-green text, or forest-green text underlined.
- A destructive confirm is the solid red pill, only where the effect is restated beside it.

## Where it appears

- `dashboard`, `onboarding`, `sign-in`, `empty-state`, `errors`, `reading` (the header's Register),
  `pricing` (on the lime band the pill is forest green with lime text).

## Variations and triggers

- **Settings and lists** have no lime pill when no action leads.
- **On the lime band** (marketing), the main pill turns forest green with lime text, so it shows on
  the lime.

## What it does not do

- No second lime pill in a window.
- No white text on lime, no lime text on white.
- No square or outlined primary buttons.

## Why

- `inferred`: lime is loud on white, so one is enough to lead the eye; a second would split it.
