# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **Black pills or light pills for money moves?** Older app screenshots show "Add money" and "Withdraw" as black pills; the app screen on the current public home page shows them as light pills on white. The library keeps the black pair. Which is current, and is black kept for one action a screen?
2. **What does the signed-in web app look like?** The sign-in page answered with a bot check, so it was not studied; the side column, the top bar and sign-in itself are inferred from the phone app.
3. **App page titles: 40px regular or 32px medium?** The help centre's titles are 32px at 500; the app's large titles on a phone are smaller still. The library uses 40px regular in the app. Which should a desktop screen use?
4. **Hairlines between rows?** The help centre and older app cards split rows with hairlines; the newest app screens group rows by space alone. The library keeps hairlines. Is space alone the direction?
5. **Toasts.** The library floats a toast only for news from elsewhere and shows failures in the page. Does the app use a notification card inside the product at all?
6. **Links: green or ink?** The stylesheets declare a green link; the help centre's links are ink, medium and underlined. The library uses green in the app's prose and ink on help pages. Is there one rule?
7. **Field corners.** The help centre's search is a pill; the library's fields have 12px corners (as the app's amount tiles). Should every field be a pill?
8. **How loud can a public page be?** Marketing pages fill the first screen with the green or with pictures; the library's pricing page is white with one green card. Should a public page open on the green?
9. **Dark mode.** Beyond black screens with light-grey pills, the app's dark mode wasn't seen; panel greys and text steps are chosen. Are they close?
10. **Destructive actions.** None was seen. The library uses red ghost text in settings and a red pill to confirm. Which does the product use?
