# Never

What Volt refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source's screens (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen, `told` is a decision by the person who commissioned the language. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never a shadow on app content.** Cards are white on the grey screen with no edge and no shadow; only menus, dialogs and a toast float. `observed` in the app's screenshots (earlier study) and on the help centre, where the rows' white cards sit flat on a grey band; the only shadows measured on the source's pages are the help centre's bar and footer (`structure.elevated` 0 on its marketing pages; 0.06–0.4 on the 18 help-centre screen-widths, from those two). The language: 0 on 24 of 27 screen-widths; the other 3 are the reading page, whose help bar and footer cast the same faint shadow.
2. **Never a fourth weight.** 400, 500 and 700; never 600. `observed`: the source's pages use 2 or 3 weights (`type.weights` 2 on 38 of 57 studied screen-widths, 3 on 19). The language: 3 on 20 of 27, 2 on 7.
3. **Never a square button.** Every button, chip, nav item and segment is a pill. `observed`: pills on every studied page (marketing, help centre, not-found).
4. **Never green body text or a green action inside the app in light mode.** The action is black; green is the brand moment. `observed`: black pills on the help centre and not-found pages; green only on the wordmark and the marketing call to join. `inferred` for the app.
5. **Never a bold headline, never a light amount.** Headlines are regular (400) and tight; amounts 700. `observed`: the marketing headlines at 400 and the app's balance in bold on the public home page.
6. **Never red for money going out.** Out is plain black; in is green with a plus. `observed` in the app screenshots (earlier study).
7. **Never a floating toast for a failure on the current screen.** It is a red notice in the page. `inferred` from refusal 1.
8. **Never a giant error number or an illustration on an error page.** Not found is a 32px title, one line and one black pill. `observed`: the source's two not-found pages have exactly those three parts (one adds a decorative illustration below, which the language leaves out: no pictures, `told`).
9. **Never outlined cards in the app.** Outlines are for marketing plan cards and stat tiles. `observed`: no outlined panels in the app screenshots; outlined tiles on the marketing footer (earlier study).
10. **Never a photo, logo, illustration or app screenshot in a recipe.** `told`: the commission (copy the system, never the screen). The source leads with pictures on many marketing pages (`media.role` fullBleed, inset or leads on 19 of 57 studied screen-widths).
11. **Never the side column on a public page, never a public bar in the app.** `observed`: the public pages carry their own bars; the app is a separate frame (`inferred` for its desktop form).
