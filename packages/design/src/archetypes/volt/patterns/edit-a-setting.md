# Edit a setting

**The job:** let someone see a value and change it.

## What Volt does

1. **The profile on top,** centred: large round initials, the name at 32px regular, the handle in
   a muted line, two small grey pills.
2. **Groups under 24px medium section titles,** each a white card of rows.
3. **Each row:** a 40px grey round icon, a medium title over a muted line of help, then at the end
   one of:
   - a **chevron** (and the current value in muted text) when it opens something;
   - a **switch** (black when on) when it takes effect at once, with no Save;
4. **The theme** as a segmented pill in its own small card.
5. **At the end,** centred: a grey "Sign out" pill and red ghost text "Close account".

## Where it appears

- `settings`. The same rows in `sign-in` (Other ways in).
- Source: `inferred`. The real settings screen wasn't in the public screenshots; the rows follow
  the app's grey-circle rows on its savings and family screens (earlier study) and the help
  centre's rows (`observed`, 57px, chevrons).

## Variations and triggers

- **A value with many options** → a chevron row that opens a list.
- **On or off** → a switch, saved as it flips.
- **Destructive** → red ghost text at the end, confirmed in a dialog with a red pill.

## What it does not do

- No Save button for switches.
- No form of inputs in a settings page; editing opens its own screen or dialog.
- No outlined groups; groups are white cards on grey.

## Why

- `inferred`: settings in a phone money app are a list of rows; the desktop keeps the rows and
  groups them.
