# Commit to the main action

**The job:** make the one thing to do now obvious and easy to press.

## What Volt does

- **A black pill with white text** (green with black text in dark), 48px, 16px medium.
- **Full width** under a form or in a card (`lg`, 56px, for the one action on a first-run step);
  otherwise sized to its label.
- **The grey pill** beside it for the other choice; a **ghost** (text only) for Back.
- **An equal pair of black pills** when money can go two ways ("Add funds", "Cash out").
- **On the green** (keypad, picked plan): the pill turns black with green text.

## Where it appears

- Every recipe: the balance card and keypad (`dashboard`), the receipt (`list-detail`), Next
  (`onboarding`), Send my code (`sign-in`), Start a goal (`empty-state`), plan buttons (`pricing`),
  Send and Back to Overview (`errors`), Join in the help bar (`reading`).
- Source: `observed`: black pills on the help centre (Join, the personalised-help Log in, Back to
  Help Center) and both not-found pages; green and black pills on marketing pages. The app's black
  pills are `observed` in the App Store screenshots (earlier study).

## Variations and triggers

- **Public marketing pages** → the green pill is the call to join; black for log in.
- **Small, inside cards and bars** → `sm`, 36px.

## What it does not do

- No square or slightly rounded buttons: always pills.
- No green action in light mode inside the app.
- No more than one black pill per job (the top bar's "Send" aside).

## Why

- `observed`: the source's actions are pills in black, green or grey on every page studied.
