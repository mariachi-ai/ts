# Choose an option

**The job:** pick one or several of a few options.

## What Flint does

- **Several from a list:** a box of one-line rows, each a 14px checkbox, the name at 13px and its
  detail in muted 12px at the right; a header line with the source and "2 of 5 selected".
- **One of a few, in a tool:** a small select sized to its value (the model in the composer, quiet
  hours in settings).
- **One of two, on a public page:** a pill track on the tinted step holding a raised pill ("Monthly",
  "Yearly").
- **A mode:** a tinted pill chip in the composer ("Draft").

## Where it appears

- `onboarding` (the repository picker), `list-detail` (the composer's mode and model), `settings`
  (selects), `pricing` (the billing toggle).

## Variations and triggers

- **Checked:** the checkbox is inverted (light on dark, dark on light).
- **Many options:** the select opens a raised menu with 26px rows and keys at the right.

## What it does not do

- No large option cards with pictures; no radio cards.
- No coloured fill for the chosen row: only its control changes.
- No pills inside the app except mode and suggestion chips.

## Why

- `inferred`: choices in the source's app are small controls in rows; the pill toggle is its public
  site's (observed on the pricing page).
