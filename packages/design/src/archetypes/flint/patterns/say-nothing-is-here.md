# Say nothing is here

**The job:** tell someone a place is empty, why, and what to do first.

## What Flint does

Centred in the work area, under the usual title bar:

1. A 40px thin icon in subtle grey.
2. A 14px heading at 500 that says what is empty and for how long ("No incidents in the last 30
   days").
3. One muted 13px line saying what will appear here and what the product can do with it.
4. One inverted action and one secondary.
5. Key hints below in muted 12px ("⌘N New task · ⌘K Commands").

## Where it appears

- `empty-state`.

## Variations and triggers

- **Empty because of a filter:** the line says so and the action clears the filter.
- **Empty for good reason:** the heading says the good news plainly; the action sets up the next thing.

## What it does not do

- No illustration, no picture, no mascot.
- No large or bold heading; no exclamation marks.
- No more than two actions.

## Why

- `inferred`: an empty place in a tool is a moment to teach the next step and its key. The source's
  not-found pages are as quiet (the largest text 1.0 to 1.29 times the body), observed.
