# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **What do the signed-in screens measure?** The app is behind a download and a sign-in; its layout
   follows public screenshots and the product frames the public site draws, and its row heights, title
   sizes and widths are the language's own tokens, not measurements. Measurements of the real task
   view, settings and dashboard would confirm or correct them.
2. **How large is an app page's title?** The source's public pages set their largest text at about 2.3
   times the body (`type.headingRatio` 2.27 [1.78, 2.57]); the language's app pages use a 22px title
   over 13px text (1.69) and the task view none. Is the app's title nearer 20px, or as large as the docs'?
3. **Is 500 the app's emphasis weight?** The source's docs bold words at 700; the language bolds at 500
   so that every page keeps two weights. Which does the app use for a box title or a bold word?
4. **Upper-case group labels?** The product frame on the source's home page labels its task groups in
   small upper-case letters followed by a count; the language uses sentence case. Is that the app's
   current habit, or only the frame's?
5. **Two-line rows in the task list?** The same frame shows each task with a muted second line (its
   last step); the language keeps side-panel rows to one line. Which is the app's?
6. **Are checked checkboxes inverted?** The language draws them in the primary's colours, so a picker
   with two checked rows shows three inverted controls. Does the app use a quieter check?
7. **The current docs page:** the language marks it in the link colour with no fill, as the source's docs
   do, while the app's current row is a grey step. Should the app follow the docs?
8. **Does a flow have a box?** Onboarding and sign-in sit on the panel colour without a card. Is the
   real sign-in a card, and does it carry a "last used" mark on one way in?
9. **A footer on public pages:** the source's marketing pages end with a footer; the recipes have none.
   Should `pricing` and `reading` end with one?
10. **Light in the app:** the language's light theme follows the public site (warm off-white); the app's
    own light theme is neutral. Which should an app screen use in light?
