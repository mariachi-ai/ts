# Never

What Halcyon refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source's public
screens (its public product demo and site; ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred`
is a reading of what was seen. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never a bold title or a bold figure in the app.** Titles and large figures are 380, running text
   400. `observed`: page titles and the balance at 28px/380 on every measured app screen; the
   heaviest app weight is 480, only on 12px labels (type.weights 4 [3, 5] over 66 screens).
2. **Never more than four weights on a screen, never 600 or 700.** `observed`: 360, 380, 400 and 480
   in the app; 360, 400, 420 and 480 on public pages.
3. **Never red for money going out, or for failure.** Out is ink with a minus; failure is magenta.
   `observed` in the transactions table (outgoing in ink with "−", a struck-through amount with a
   magenta "Failed" badge).
4. **Never a solid accent pill on a list page or in settings.** The page's own action is a neutral pill.
   `observed`: the transactions and recipients pages act through neutral pills and settings through
   accent-text "Edit ›" links; the solid accent appears for sending money on the home page and at the
   end of flows.
5. **Never a square button.** Every button is a pill. `observed`: 9999px radius on every measured
   button, including split buttons.
6. **Never a frame or a filled header on a table.** `observed` on the transactions, cards, invoices and
   accounts tables: hairline rows, 12–13px muted headers on white.
7. **Never icons larger than about 13px in navigation, buttons or the top bar.** `observed`: icons.size
   12 [10, 14] over 63 screens; sidebar icons 12px, button icons 11px.
8. **Never leave the app frame for an empty or error state, and never an icon or picture in one.**
   `observed`: the app's not-found page and a filtered-to-nothing transactions page keep the sidebar and
   top bar and show a centred 17px line and one sentence, with no icon.
9. **Never a sidebar on a public page, or a site bar in the app.** `observed`: the site's pages carry
   only the site bar and footer; the app's pages only the sidebar and top bar.
10. **Never a coloured side stripe, a border on a card, or a heavy shadow.** Cards float on a ring
    shadow; alerts are tinted with no border. `observed` (computed styles in the earlier study, private
    notes) and on the measured app screens.
