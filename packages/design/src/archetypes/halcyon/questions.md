# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **What does opening an account look like?** The source's sign-up is not public (it refuses automated
   visits), so `onboarding` follows the public log-in page's frame (pale ground, one white card) and the
   app's first-step pages (28px/380 title). How many questions does one step ask, and is there a list
   of steps beside it?
2. **Is 400 the app's running-text weight everywhere?** The measured app pages set rows, nav and values
   at 400 and keep 360 for buttons and the top bar; the earlier notes had 360 as the body weight from
   the public pages' ledes. Is any app text meant to be 360?
3. **How deep is the app?** The source's app pages measure two levels of separated boxes
   (structure.depth 2), the recipes one. Is there a box inside a box on an ordinary app page, or does
   the second level come from the demo's own panels?
4. **The public headline face:** journal headlines on the source use a serif; the language uses its
   display face at 480. Should `reading` carry a serif (an open one, such as Newsreader)?
5. **The sidebar on a phone:** the source hides it behind a menu button; the recipes, which run without
   script, put the page first and the sidebar below. Is a menu button the expected way in?
6. **Empty states with a first action:** the source's recipients page adds through a neutral split pill;
   is a brand-new area ever given a solid accent pill to start, or always the neutral one?
7. **Pictures on public pages:** the source's journal and product pages lead with large images; the
   language ships none. What should a builder put there: nothing, a product frame drawn in HTML, or a
   flat tint?
8. **Whole-page errors:** the app's not-found page has no action at all; the recipes give one quiet pill.
   Should it stay with none?
9. **Icons at 11 or 12px:** buttons measure 11px and the sidebar 12px on the source; the language uses
   12 for both. Is the smaller button icon deliberate?
