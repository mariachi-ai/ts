# Never

What Cadence refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source's studied screens (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never the green for links, headings, decoration or errors.** Green is the play button, the main pill, switches and checks that are on, what is playing, and the mark. Links are white or grey, headings white, errors red. `observed`: on every studied web player screen the only green is the play button and the mark; on the site, the header's pill; on the settings screen, switches that are on.
2. **Never a rule between song rows.** One rule under the header row; rows are separated by space and the hover tint. `observed`: the record and playlist screens (56px rows, no rules). The exception is episodes, whose taller rows with a description are split by hairlines (`observed` on a show screen).
3. **Never a page that scrolls in the app.** The frame is the window's height, the panels scroll inside it, and the bar at the bottom stays put. `observed`: on the web player the document is exactly the window's height at 1440 and at 390, and the main view scrolls inside itself.
4. **Never a square button.** Every button and chip is a pill; icon buttons are circles. `observed`: every button on every studied screen (header pills, Follow, Edit, Import library, the prompt boxes' pills, chips).
5. **Never a border around a panel or a card.** Areas are set apart by their tint and the black frame's 8px gaps. `observed`: over the studied screens' separated areas, 87% are tint and 1% a border (`structure.separation`).
6. **Never a white or coloured header bar.** The top bar and the site header are black; colour comes only from a page's art, as the wash behind its header. `observed`: every studied screen.
7. **Never a middling page title on one thing.** The title of a record, mix, show or performer is the largest type in the product (96px at 800, shrinking for long titles); everything else is 24px or smaller. `observed`: the record, playlist, show and performer screens.
8. **Never a fourth weight.** 400 for text, 700 for labels, buttons and titles, 800 for the huge titles. `observed`: two or three weights on every studied screen (`type.weights` 2 [2, 3]).
9. **Never uppercase,** except the site footer's 12px column titles. `observed`: no uppercase headings or buttons on any studied screen (`words.case`); the site footer's column titles are uppercase and tracked.
10. **Never a frame on cover art, and never art without its corners:** 6px for covers, 4px in rows, circles for people. `observed`: every studied shelf and list.
11. **Never a spinner over a page or a shelf.** Loading is grey blocks in the art's shape. `inferred`: no spinner was seen on any studied screen; the published skeleton rule (private notes).
12. **Never light first.** The product is dark on every screen; the light palette is the published one and is offered, not led with. `observed`: every studied screen is dark.
