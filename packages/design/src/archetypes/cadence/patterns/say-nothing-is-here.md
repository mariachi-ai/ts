# Say nothing is here

**The job:** tell someone a place is empty, why, and give them the one way to fill it.

## What Cadence does

1. The page's chips stay (the filter that found nothing).
2. Centred in the panel: a 48px glyph in an 88px disc on the highlight grey, a bold 32px title that says what will appear here ("Shows you save land here"), one 16px sentence, then a white pill (the way to fill it) and an outlined one (another way in).
3. Under it, **prompt boxes**: the highlight grey, 8px corners, a bold 16px title, one 14px line and a small white pill.

## Where it appears

- `empty-state`.
- Source: observed: the prompt boxes ("make your first …", "find something to follow") fill the collection panel of every signed-out web player screen. The centred block follows the published empty-state component (private notes), not a public screen.

## Variations and triggers

- **An empty collection panel** shows only the prompt boxes, stacked.
- **A search with nothing found** keeps the query in the title and offers to check the spelling (inferred: every studied search returned results).

## What it does not do

- No illustration, no mascot, no green: the disc holds a plain grey-white glyph and the pill is white.
- No "Nothing here" without a way out.
- No skeleton for an empty place; skeletons are only for loading.

## Why

- `inferred`: an empty collection is an invitation to start listening, so the screen leads straight to browsing.
