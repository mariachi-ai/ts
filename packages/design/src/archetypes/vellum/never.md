# Never

What Vellum refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source's screens (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen, `told` is a decision by the person who commissioned the language. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never a shadow on content.** Panels are set apart by a grey fill (or a white panel on grey), never a drop shadow; only menus, sheets, dialogs and tooltips float. `observed`: no shadow on app content in the source's desktop and phone screenshots (earlier study). The language: `structure.elevated` 0 on all 27 recipe screen-widths. The source's public help-category cards do cast shadows (`structure.elevated` up to 0.84 on those pages); that is its website, not its app.
2. **Never a fourth weight.** 400 for text, 600 for labels, tabs, list titles and buttons, 700 for headings. `observed`: the source's pages use 2 or 3 weights (36 and 24 of 60 studied screen-widths); the language 3 on 26 of 27 (one phone window shows 2).
3. **Never outlined white boxes for grouped rows,** never a border on each row. Groups are grey grouped panels with inset dividers. `observed`: the phone app's settings and the Today screen's Moments list (screenshots). The web app's settings modal draws one outlined group; see questions.md.
4. **Never white text on the sky blue.** Text on the brand colour is ink. `inferred` from contrast: white on `#44c0ff` is 2.1:1 (the source does it; the library holds 4.5:1).
5. **Never a sky-blue primary.** Actions use the deep blue `#1e75ae`. `observed`: the site's own button blue; the sky is the brand and the journals' colour.
6. **Never a square button in the app.** App buttons and segmented controls are pills; public help pages use 4px corners. `observed`: pills in the app screenshots and on the plans page (49px, 30px corners); 0–5px buttons on the help and gift pages.
7. **Never a card around an entry or a timeline row.** Rows are split by hairlines and grouped by grey month bands. `observed` in the desktop and phone timelines (screenshots).
8. **Never colour for decoration or for status in the timeline.** Colour says which journal. `observed`: journal colours on swatches, bands, the open row and calendar days.
9. **Never a toast floating over writing in a page shape.** Notices sit in the page as tinted callouts with one action. `inferred` from refusal 1.
10. **Never a photo, logo, app screenshot or illustration in a recipe.** Pictures are token shapes (a pale field, a sun, two hills). `told`: the commission (copy the system, never the screen). The source leads with pictures on 13 of 60 studied screen-widths (`media.role` leads).
11. **Never the journal column on a public page, never the public header in the app.** `observed`: the site's header on public pages; the column in the app.
