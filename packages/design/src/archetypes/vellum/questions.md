# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **Grey panels or outlined groups in settings?** The phone app's settings set white groups apart from a grey ground; the web app's settings modal draws one outlined white group on white. The library uses the grey grouped panel everywhere (as on the Today screen). Which is newer, and should the web settings be outlined?
2. **What do the signed-in web screens look like today?** The web app was not opened; its screenshots in the help centre may be out of date. The timeline, settings and errors in the web form are inferred from the desktop and phone apps.
3. **How does the app show trouble?** No sync error, undo or failed save was seen. The library shows them as tinted notices in the page (no floating toast). Does the app use a banner, a toast, or a status line in the column?
4. **Shadows on public help pages.** The help centre's category cards cast shadows; the app has none. Should a public list page (a help index) keep the shadowed cards, or stay flat like the app?
5. **Square or rounded on public pages?** The site's help and gift buttons have 0–5px corners, its plan buttons are pills, its download buttons 10px. The library uses 4px on help pages and pills elsewhere. Is there a rule?
6. **Destructive actions.** None was seen. The library confirms deletion with a red pill; the phone platform's habit is red text in a row. Which does the product use?
7. **Text on the journal colour.** The app sets white titles on the sky-blue band and the open row; the library uses ink for contrast. Is ink acceptable on the band?
8. **Month bands.** The phone app groups the timeline under grey month bands; the desktop app uses a bold month heading without a band. The library uses the band in both. Is the desktop's plain heading deliberate?
9. **Dark mode.** "Pure black" comes from the product's own release note; no current dark screenshot was found. Are panels `#1c1c1e` on black, as the platform's are?
10. **Pictures.** The source's pages often lead with a photo or an app screenshot; the language uses none (commission). Should public pages get a token-drawn picture shape that leads?
