# Commit to the main action

**The job:** make the one thing to do next obvious.

## What Vellum does

- **One primary pill per view,** in the deep action blue with white text, weight 600: 48px and full width at the foot of a form, a sheet or a question; 40px at the right of a form's buttons.
- **The second action** is a ghost (blue text, no fill) under it, or a grey pill beside it.
- **Everything else** is a grey pill or a plain glyph.

## Where it appears

- `dashboard` (Write about it), `onboarding` (Continue), `empty-state` (Write the first letter), `sign-in` (Sign in), `errors` (Make journal; Go to your journals), `pricing` (Try it free), `reading` (Search, Send a message: small-cornered on the public help page).
- Source: a full-width blue pill under a question is `observed` on the phone Today screen (earlier study); pills on the plans page and small-cornered blue buttons on the help pages are `observed` on the studied site (private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- **A form in the app** → Cancel (grey) then the primary, at the right.
- **A question or a sheet** → full width, the ghost under it.
- **A public help page** → 4px corners, as the site's help buttons are.

## What it does not do

- No second primary in the same window; two plans' pills on the pricing page are the exception, as the source's plans page shows.
- No sky-blue primary (white on sky is 2.1:1).
- No shadows or gradients on buttons.

## Why

- `observed`: the source's pill actions and its one bright action per screen.
- `inferred`: a journal asks for one thing at a time.
