# Move between areas

**The job:** get from one part of the product to another.

## What Vellum does

- **In the app, the journal column** (15rem, cool grey `#e2e7ea`, no edge line): the wordmark with a round settings button; the app's views with 15px icons and right-aligned counts; a hairline; "Journals" with a square colour swatch per journal and a count; "Start a journal" with a plus; a hairline; Tags and Archive. The current row is a darker grey with 8px corners.
- **In settings,** a second grey column of sections; the current one fills with the action blue, white text.
- **On public pages,** a white header: the wordmark at the left, 17px ink links, the current one in accent text, and one outlined small-cornered (4px) Download button at the right.
- **On a phone,** the journal column stacks above the page.

## Where it appears

- The column: `dashboard`, `list-detail`, `settings`, `empty-state`, `errors`. The public header: `pricing`, `reading`. Neither: `onboarding`, `sign-in` (the brand band with the wordmark).
- Source: the grey journal column with swatches and counts is `observed` in the desktop app screenshot; the public header with plain links and an outlined button on the studied site pages (private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- **Inside a journal** → its row is current; the page may open with its colour band.
- **Settings** → the settings button by the wordmark is pressed; the section list appears.

## What it does not do

- No top navigation bar inside the app; no journal column on public pages.
- No "all entries" row; no icons for journals (a swatch stands for each).
- No drawer on a phone.

## Why

- `observed`: the desktop app's three panes and the site's header.
- `inferred`: stacking the column on a phone keeps one way of moving instead of two.
