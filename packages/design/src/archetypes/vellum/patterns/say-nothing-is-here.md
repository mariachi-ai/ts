# Say nothing's here

**The job:** tell someone a place is empty and give them the first step.

## What Vellum does

1. The page keeps its frame: the journal column, the journal's colour band and 34px title, the text tabs.
2. In the middle, centred in a 30rem column: a 40px outline icon in the subtle colour, a 20px bold heading, one sentence in muted text (at most 24rem), then two stacked pills: the primary and a grey one.

## Where it appears

- `empty-state` (a new journal).
- Source: not seen publicly; built from the language's own parts (`inferred`).

## Variations and triggers

- **A new journal** → Write the first entry; start from a question.
- **A search with no results** → the same block, one grey pill to clear the search.

## What it does not do

- No illustration or photo.
- No hiding the tabs or the title.
- No more than two actions.

## Why

- `inferred`: the empty page should still look like the place it will become.
