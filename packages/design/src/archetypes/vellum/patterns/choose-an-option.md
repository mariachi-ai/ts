# Choose an option

**The job:** pick one from a few: a colour, a plan, a view.

## What Vellum does

- **A colour:** a row of 36px colour squares (8px corners) with a radio above and the name below; the chosen radio fills with the control blue.
- **A plan:** white plan cards (20px corners, a hairline) on a grey band, a 24px icon and a 20px bold name, a 48px bold price with "per year", a 13px tick list, and a 48px pill at the foot; the featured plan is ringed in amber with a "Most chosen" badge.
- **A view:** a plain toolbar of 18px glyphs, the current one on a grey pill; or text tabs, grey until current, the current in ink with a bar under it.

## Where it appears

- `onboarding` (colour), `pricing` (plans and the comparison table), `list-detail` (view glyphs), `empty-state` (text tabs).
- Source: plan cards with 49px pill buttons and a comparison table are `observed` on the studied plans page; the view glyphs and text tabs in the earlier study's app screenshots (private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- **Three or more plans** → cards in a grid, then a comparison table with 13px capital column labels.
- **Two to five views** → glyphs in a toolbar, or text tabs under a journal band.

## What it does not do

- No dropdown for a choice of colour.
- No primary pill on every plan: the free plan's pill is outlined.
- No icons on text tabs except the last "about" glyph.

## Why

- `observed`: the plans page's cards and buttons; the app's segmented view controls.
- `inferred`: colour is how a journal is recognised, so it is picked by sight.
