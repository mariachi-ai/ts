# Show progress

**The job:** show how far someone is through a flow, or how much they have written.

## What Vellum does

- **In a flow:** "Step 2 of 3" in semibold on the colour band above the title, then a 6px rounded bar in the control blue on the grey track at the top of the sheet.
- **Writing so far:** the month calendar with written days filled in the journal's colour and today ringed in the accent, "16 days written" beside the month; grey tiles with 11px capital labels over 20px numerals; a streak tile with a big 34px numeral.

## Where it appears

- `onboarding` (the step and the bar), `dashboard` (calendar, counts, streak).
- Source: the filled calendar and the stat tiles are `observed` in the earlier study's desktop and phone screenshots; the flow's bar is `inferred`.

## Variations and triggers

- **A flow of three steps or fewer** → the step line and the bar.
- **Habits** → the calendar first, counts after.

## What it does not do

- No charts with gridlines; no percentages for writing.
- No numbered step circles.

## Why

- `observed`: the source shows writing as a filled calendar and counts.
- `inferred`: a streak is encouragement, so it is a number, not a target.
