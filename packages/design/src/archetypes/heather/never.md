# Never

What Heather refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source's public
screens (its public video pages, its site and its not-found pages; ids in the private notes) or a
measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen, `told` is a rule someone set. The recipes
follow every one.

1. **Never a fourth weight, never 600.** Text 400, controls and labels 500, headings 653. `observed`:
   the public video page's window uses exactly 400, 500 and 653 (type.weights 3 [2, 3] over 24
   detail screen-widths; the site's pages use two). The recipes measure 3 [3, 3] on all 27.
2. **Never a second accent.** One blue for the solid button, links, the current tab and times;
   other colour only for status. `observed`: one saturated hue on the source's pages (colour.hues
   1 [0, 1] over 57 screen-widths).
3. **Never grey timestamps.** A time is blue, because it jumps to that moment. `observed`: the
   transcript's times are 12px blue on every studied video page.
4. **Never bold or colour the words of the playing line.** Only its background (pale blue) and a
   1px blue edge change. `observed` on the video pages' transcripts.
5. **Never a drop shadow on a resting card or panel.** Hairlines and 16px corners; only what floats
   lifts (the reaction bar, the comment box, menus, flags, dialogs). `observed` for the floating
   reaction bar and comment box on the video pages; `inferred` for resting cards, from public
   screenshots of the library.
6. **Never pills inside the app.** App buttons have 12px corners; pills only on the public site.
   `observed`: pill buttons (9999px) on the pricing page; `inferred` for the app's 12px, estimated
   from public screenshots, not measured.
7. **Never more than two solid blue buttons in view.** One belongs to the frame (the sidebar's record
   button, or the site bar's primary) and at most one to the page. `observed`: never more than two
   primaries in a window of the source (none on 10, one on 18, two on 29 of 57 measured
   screen-widths); `inferred` for the record button, from public screenshots of the library.
8. **Never an illustration or picture in an empty or error state.** An outline icon in a round badge
   is the only image. `told`: the language never copies illustrations (the source's own not-found
   page has one; this is a deliberate difference).
9. **Never a sidebar on a public page, or the site bar in the app.** `observed`: every studied public
   page carries only the site bar and footer; `inferred` for the app frame.
10. **Never uppercase, except tiny tags on the public site.** Sentence case for headings, buttons and
    labels. `observed`: the pricing page's two 12px uppercase tags are its only uppercase text.
