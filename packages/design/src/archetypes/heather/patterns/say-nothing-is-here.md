# Say nothing is here

**The job:** tell someone a place is empty and give them the one way to fill it.

## What Heather does

1. **The frame and the title stay:** sidebar, top bar, breadcrumb, the 32px title and the page's
   secondary action ("Add people").
2. **A soft grey well** (the sunken background, 16px corners, no border) fills the content column,
   with generous space above and below.
3. **Centred in it,** at most 28rem wide: a 64px pale-blue circle with a 28px outline icon, a 20px
   heading at 653 ("No videos in this channel yet"), one 14px muted sentence saying what would be
   here and who could see it, then the primary ("Start recording") and one secondary ("Upload a
   video").

## Where it appears

- `empty-state`.

## Variations and triggers

- **Nothing matches a search:** the same well with a search icon and a secondary to clear the
  search; no primary.
- **Phone:** the well fills the width; the buttons stack.

## What it does not do

- No illustration or picture; an outline icon in a circle is the only image.
- No leaving the app frame for an empty page.
- No more than two actions.

## Why

- `inferred`: an empty place in a video product is an invitation to record, so the way out is the
  record action itself; the frame stays so the person knows where they are.
