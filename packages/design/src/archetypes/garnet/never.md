# Never

What Garnet refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source's public pages (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never a second accent for actions.** Actions are maroon pills (cream on dark); there is no blue, green or yellow button. `observed`: every action pill on every studied public page is the ink `#2e0000` or an outline in the ink at 18–20%; the footer's pair is white with maroon text.
2. **Never a square button.** Every button is a full pill. `observed`: `999px` on every header, hero, plan and footer button measured; only the product frames' tiny 22px chips have 6px corners.
3. **Never more than one heavy line in view.** The display face at 800 is for the one headline; section titles are Geist 500–600. `observed`: the display face appears once per window on every studied page, at 48–54px (68px on one closing section).
4. **Never a fifth weight.** 400, 500, 600 and the display. `observed`: `type.weights` is 4 on 38 of the 48 measured widths of the source's own pages, 5 on 7 and 6 on 3 (the home page's product frames, pricing's extra 550, the security page).
5. **Never a public page on flat white.** Public pages are the warm grey ground inside the white rim, which is fixed to the window. `observed`: `#ece9ea` sheet, white rim and header plate on every studied page except the 404, which is a plain white default.
6. **Never a heavy border or dark shadow in the product.** Panels are white with a 1px ring in `#f0f0f0` and a faint shadow. `observed`: the product frames' hairlines are 1px `#f0f0f0`/`#f4f4f4`, many drawn as inset rings; shadows at 2–8%.
7. **Never colour without meaning in the product.** Green is a good fit or done, amber a middling fit, blue is what an agent filled in. `observed`: the fit bars and the blue cells and count chips in the product frames.
8. **Never the yellow on more than one small mark.** `observed`: the yellow appears on pricing as the one saving badge and in the dot artwork, which the language does not copy.
9. **Never capitals except mono labels and the rail's eyebrow.** `observed`: `words.case` sentence 0.93, title 0.07 on the source; capitals only on the mono step labels and the tracked eyebrow over a page rail.
10. **Never an illustration on an error page.** `observed`: the site's 404 is one line with a vertical rule. `inferred` for the app.
11. **Never a footer in the app, never a sidebar on a public page.** `inferred`: the app is not public; every public page ends with the maroon footer and has no side nav.
