# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **What do the signed-in screens look like?** The app is behind a login and was not studied. Every app recipe follows the product frames drawn on the public pages (the record table, the record card, the assistant panel, the deal board). The app shell (the nav on the white rim beside a near-white sheet) is a reading of the site's own rim, not something seen. The sign-in page was not seen either: the language draws it as the onboarding door is drawn (the wordmark row, a centred display headline and lead on the warm ground, one rimmed card). Is that the real door?
2. **Does the app use the display face for page titles?** The language gives every app page a 32px display title so it keeps the source's habit of one heavy line per window. The product frames show 14–16px titles at 500. Which is right inside the app?
3. **Maroon or near-black for the app's main button?** The product frames' run button is near-black `#1a1b1f`; the site's is maroon `#2e0000`. The language uses maroon everywhere. Is the near-black intended in the app?
4. **Is there a dark mode?** None is public. The language's dark is the footer's maroon (`#2e0404`, cream text, lines at 8%); the design gallery site is near-black with a yellow accent. Which should dark follow?
5. **How is the fit bar scaled?** Observed: five green at 90–94, four green at 79–88, three amber at 66–78. The language uses 90/80/65 as the steps. Are those the real thresholds?
6. **Where does settings live?** No settings screen is public; the recipe is inferred from the docs' list of settings areas.
7. **What is the empty state's voice?** The only empty view seen is the lead-search frame before it fills (headers, one muted line, the composer). Is there a tile, title and buttons as the language draws them, or only the line?
8. **What does the app's phone layout do?** No phone app screens are public; the recipes drop the nav below the sheet.
9. **Should inputs keep the light hairline?** The source's control edges are hairlines near `#e4e4e4`; the language darkens them to the ink at 50% to meet the 3:1 floor.
10. **Accordion marker:** the source's questions end in a thin plus; the library draws a chevron. Should the component gain a plus marker?
