# Show one thing

**The job:** show one company, person or deal and why it matters, beside the list it came from.

## What Garnet does

1. A white panel beside the table (20–22rem), 20px inside.
2. A head: the name at 16px/500, the domain and size in subtle 13px under it, the fit bar with a large 24px score at the right; a muted hairline under the head.
3. The evidence as a timeline: a green check circle, then the source in subtle 12px ("Funding"), the fact in ink 13px, and the reason it matched in the text green.
4. Two pills at the foot: an outline "Open record" and a ghost "Not a fit".
5. On a reading page the one thing is a post: a page rail, the breadcrumb with the date at the right, the display title (about 50px wide, 40px at 1024, 30px on a phone), a muted lead, a rimmed picture and 16/27 prose.

## Where it appears

- `list-detail` (the side panel), `reading` (a changelog post).
- Source: `observed` on the product frames (the record card with its score and the evidence steps with green checks) and on the changelog, blog and compare pages (the "Contents" rail with a dash on the current item, the breadcrumb and date) (ids in the private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- **A full record page** (inferred): underline tabs (Overview, Activity, Notes, Tasks) under the head, the activity chart, then the feed.
- **A long post:** the rail stays at the left from 64rem up; on a phone it is dropped.

## What it does not do

- No modal for a record; it opens beside the list or as its own page.
- No evidence without its source.
- No hero picture above a post's title; the picture follows the lead.

## Why

- `inferred`: showing where each fact came from is how the product earns trust for what its agents did.
