# Move between areas

**The job:** get around the app and the public site.

## What Garnet does

1. **Public:** the header plate: white, 58px, at most 69rem, joined to the top of the white rim with 24px corners at the bottom, sticking there as the page scrolls; wordmark, 14/500 muted links, outline pill, maroon pill.
2. **App:** the nav on the white rim beside the near-white sheet: the workspace mark and name, 14px rows with 14px icons, counts in subtle text, a tracked capital "RECORDS" group, Settings at the bottom; the current row a pill on `#f1eeef`.
3. **Footer:** the maroon footer as a dark window: a framed panel with a title bar (the mark, "Log in", the cream pill) and four columns of link rows on hairlines, each column headed by an icon and a muted label.
4. **A long page:** a "Contents" rail with a dash on the current item.

## Where it appears

- Every recipe (app or public), `pricing` and `reading` (footer, rail).
- Source: `observed`: the header plate (pinned to the top of the window, with the white rim fixed around the window and the warm grey as the page's own background), the dark footer window with its link grid (`#2e0404`, lines at 8%), and the rails on every public page (ids in the private notes). The app nav is `inferred` from the docs' list of product areas.

## Variations and triggers

- **Phone:** the plate keeps the wordmark, the maroon pill and a nine-dot menu (`observed` on phone captures); the app's nav drops below the sheet.

## What it does not do

- No header that changes colour on scroll.
- No sidebar on public pages, no footer in the app.
- No underline on the current nav item.

## Why

- `inferred`: the plate and the rim make the site feel like one object; the app keeps the rim so it feels like the same object opened.
