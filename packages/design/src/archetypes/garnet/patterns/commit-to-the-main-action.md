# Commit to the main action

**The job:** make the one thing to do next obvious, and the rest quiet.

## What Garnet does

1. One maroon pill per view, 600 weight, white text; at the right end of the title row, the foot of a form, or full width in a plan card.
2. Beside it an outline pill in the ink at 20% for the alternative ("Log in", "Book a demo", "Talk to us").
3. Quiet actions are ghost text ("Back", "Cancel", "Do this later").
4. Sizes: 28px in product bars, 34px by default, 44px on public pages and plan cards.
5. In the header plate the pair is always outline then maroon at the far right.

## Where it appears

- Every recipe.
- Source: `observed` on every public page: the header's outline "Log in" and maroon pill, the hero's maroon pill beside a light outline one, the plan cards' full-width pills (measured: 999px radius, 14/600 in the header, 15.2/600 in the hero, 13.6/550 and 44px in plan cards) (ids in the private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- **Dark:** the main pill turns cream with maroon text (the footer's measured pair).
- **Inside a product frame** the source's run button is near-black (`#1a1b1f`); Garnet keeps maroon, which reads near-black at that size.
- **Destructive:** the danger pill, only in a confirm or a settings danger row.

## What it does not do

- No square or 8px-cornered buttons.
- No second maroon pill in the same view.
- No coloured buttons (blue, green, yellow).

## Why

- `inferred`: using the ink as the action colour keeps the page warm and lets the headline, not a button colour, carry the brand.
