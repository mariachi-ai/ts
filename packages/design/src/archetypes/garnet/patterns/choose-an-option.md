# Choose an option

**The job:** pick one of a few options, or move between steps.

## What Garnet does

1. A pill track: a grey `#e2dfe0` track with 3px inside, the chosen segment white with the sm shadow, 14/500, muted when not chosen.
2. A saving or a "new" rides on its segment as the one yellow mark ("−15%").
3. Steps are the same track with mono numbers: "01 Workspace ✓ · 02 Your data · 03 Mailbox · 04 Team".
4. Larger choices are white rows with 12px corners and a hairline, a radio, a title at 500 and one muted line; the chosen one takes the control edge.

## Where it appears

- `pricing` (period), `onboarding` (steps and choices).
- Source: `observed`: the pricing page's Monthly/Annual track with a yellow "−20%", the home page's numbered stage track ("01 …" to "04 …") in mono, the changelog filter track (ids in the private notes).

## Variations and triggers

- **More than five options:** a select, not a track.
- **Record views:** underline tabs instead of a track.

## What it does not do

- No toggle with a coloured fill for a period.
- No step circles or progress dots.
- No yellow on more than one mark in view.

## Why

- `inferred`: the numbered track tells a story in order, which the source uses for its four stages.
