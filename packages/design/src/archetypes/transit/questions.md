# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **What do the signed-in screens look like on a desktop?** Trips, the account and settings are inferred from the public pages, the published components and the store pages' side list. Screenshots of the account pages would confirm or correct the side list, the 72px rows and the prompt banner.
2. **Is blue a button colour or only a brand-panel colour?** The help pages put a blue sign-in button on a pale blue banner; every other studied action is black. The library allows blue only for a prompt banner's action. Is that the rule, or a help-centre exception?
3. **Blue links in articles, black links elsewhere.** Help articles link in blue; public pages, breadcrumbs and the not-found pages link in black. Which is newer, and should an app screen ever use the blue?
4. **Bordered panel or grey tile?** The booking panel has a 2px pale edge; marketing tiles and banners are grey or tinted with no edge. On the source's public pages tinted panels are the majority (`structure.tinted` typical 0.67); the recipes use more edges and hairlines (0.29). When should a new panel be a tile instead of an edged card?
5. **How big is the public headline in the app's own pages?** The source's 404 is a public page with a 52px title; the language's errors page is in the app and uses 36px. Should app error pages take the public 52px?
6. **The wordmark size.** The source's mark is a picture about 24px tall; the language's text wordmark is 28px. Is 28px right next to 36px titles?
7. **Square side lists and tabs.** The side list's rows and the header tabs have square corners while buttons have 8px, so pages with a side list measure square controls overall. Is the square side list deliberate everywhere, or only in the store's menu?
8. **Pictures.** The source leads with a photo or illustration on about a third of its first windows; the language uses none (commission). Should the language get a placeholder picture shape for public pages, drawn in tokens like the map?
9. **Dark mode.** The public dark page studied earlier is true black; the published components' dark grounds are `#161616`. Is the app's dark mode true black too?
