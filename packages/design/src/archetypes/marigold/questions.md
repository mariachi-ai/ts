# Marigold: questions

At most ten. Each is a reason or a conflict that the library can't settle on its own; the answer
becomes a `told` entry where it lands. Where the library had to choose meanwhile, the choice is named
and marked `inferred`.

1. **Disabled buttons.** The source draws a disabled action as a grey face with a grey label, and
   keeps it in place until an answer is picked; the library draws disabled as half opacity. Should
   Marigold's disabled primary be the grey face instead?
2. **Account screens.** On the source's public log-in form the full-width action is its sky blue and
   the fields are labelled only by their placeholders. Marigold chose the green primary (one primary
   variant; blue means chosen) and visible labels above every field (accessibility), as the
   `sign-in` recipe shows. Keep both choices, or follow the source on the account screens?
3. **Green words.** The source sets its big public headlines in its bright green (2.1:1 on white).
   Marigold uses a darker text green (`#3b7700`, 5.5:1) and puts the landing footer's links in dark
   ink on the green band instead of the source's white. Is the darker green acceptable as the
   language's headline colour, or should big headlines be ink (as the source's blog does on phones)?
4. **The person's own row in a ranking.** Public screenshots of the source show it in the pale grey
   well; the recipe used the pale green with green text until an earlier pass. Which is the habit now?
5. **Headline case.** Settled for display headlines: case follows the face (green display face
   lowercase, ink text face sentence case). Still open: the source sets its help and legal page
   titles in title case, and on phones its blog post moves the picture above the headline and sets
   the headline in ink at sentence case. Marigold uses sentence case and one order everywhere. Copy
   either habit?
6. **Pictures in the app.** Pictures are settled for public pages (four slots, the product's own
   pictures or a tinted stand-in). Should an app screen ever lead with the product's own picture (a
   unit's illustration, a person's photo on their profile), or does the icon in a pale circle stay
   the only stand-in inside the app?
7. **Pricing on white.** The source's plan comparison sits on a dark sub-brand. Marigold puts it on
   the core white brand. Should the paid plan get its own dark treatment?
8. **Settings.** No public settings screen of the source was found; the recipe is assembled from its
   form, card and shell rules. Is the right-column menu card where settings navigation belongs?
9. **Phones.** The source's app has an icon-only bottom tab bar on phones (the current tab in the sky
   tint with a blue outline); the library stacks the side column above the content. Should Marigold
   recipes draw a bottom bar on phones?
10. **Where the green ends.** Settled for actions: one green action in any window, repeated at most
    once at a long page's close. Still open: the source fills its unit banners and path steps with
    the same bright green as the action. Is there a limit on how much of one screen may be green
    fill?
