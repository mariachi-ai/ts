# Marigold: questions

At most ten. Each is a reason or a conflict that the library can't settle on its own; the answer
becomes a `told` entry where it lands.

1. **Disabled buttons.** The source draws a disabled action as a grey face with a grey label, and
   keeps it in place until an answer is picked; the library draws disabled as half opacity. Should
   Marigold's disabled primary be the grey face instead?
2. **The sign-in form's action colour.** On the source's public log-in form the full-width action
   is the sky blue, while every other primary seen is the green. Is blue the rule for account
   screens (sign in, recover, sign up), or an exception Marigold should not copy?
3. **Labels on sign-in fields.** The source's log-in fields are labelled only by their placeholders;
   Marigold's fields keep a label above. Keep the labels (accessibility) or follow the source on the
   narrow account screens only?
4. **The person's own row in a ranking.** Public screenshots of the source show it in the pale grey
   well; the recipe used the pale green with green text until this pass. Which is the habit now?
5. **Lowercase headlines.** The source sets some hero headlines in lowercase (the display face) and
   others in sentence case (front page, course pages), and its help and legal titles in title case.
   What decides lowercase: the display face, the page, or the size?
6. **Pictures.** The source leans on character illustrations: most of its public screens lead with
   one. Marigold ships none and uses an icon in a pale circle. Is that the accepted stand-in, or
   should screens that would lead with a picture keep a picture slot (a product photo, a user's
   own image)?
7. **Pricing on white.** The source's plan comparison sits on a dark sub-brand. Marigold puts it on
   the core white brand. Should the paid plan get its own dark treatment?
8. **Settings.** No public settings screen of the source was found; the recipe is assembled from its
   form, card and shell rules. Is the right-column menu card where settings navigation belongs?
9. **Phones.** The source's app has an icon-only bottom tab bar on phones (the current tab in the sky
   tint with a blue outline); the library stacks the side column above the content. Should Marigold
   recipes draw a bottom bar on phones?
10. **Where the green ends.** The source fills its unit banners, path steps and the primary with the
    same bright green, and its public marketing uses it for headlines too. Is there a limit on how
    much of one screen may be green, or is "one green action, plus progress" the whole rule?
