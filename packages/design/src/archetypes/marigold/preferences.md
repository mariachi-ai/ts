# Marigold: preferences

What people show they want as they work with this language: a correction to a screen, a pick between
two versions, or a check message accepted on purpose. Newest first. Nothing is recorded yet.

Each entry keeps its reach. If the reach isn't clear, record the narrowest and ask. A preference that
conflicts with the library keeps both entries, marked `conflict`; the newer one, from the person
closest to the design, wins until someone settles it.

## Format

```md
### YYYY-MM-DD · <short name>

- **What changed:** <the before and the after, in measures where there is one: "row height 56px → 64px">
- **Who:** <the person> · **told** (or **observed**: they picked it; **accepted**: they kept a flagged screen)
- **Screen:** <recipe or screen id>
- **Reach:** this screen | this kind (<kind>) | everywhere
- **Conflicts with:** <the library entry it overrides, or "none">
```
