# Never

What Terracotta refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source screens (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never the brand colour for selection, focus, links or headings.** Current tabs carry a 2px ink bar, links are ink and underlined, the focus ring is ink. `observed`: the category tabs on the browse screens, the audience tabs on the help home, the `Share` and `Save` links on the place pages and the `Browse all topics` link (private notes); the published focus border is ink. The one coloured link seen is legal small print (blue), which the language does not carry.
2. **Never a frame or shadow around a photo card.** The photo has rounded corners and the text sits under it on the page. `observed`: the guide cards on the help home and the article cards on the resource pages.
3. **Never two gradient actions in one view.** `observed`: each studied screen that shows the gradient action shows one (the place pages' booking panel, the log-in sheet, the help home's panel, the city landing's search card).
4. **Never a box around a page section.** Sections are opened by a full-width hairline and space. `observed`: over the studied source screens, separated containers are dividers 43% of the time and fully bordered 10%; 95% of collections are separated by space alone (`structure.separation`).
5. **Never a coloured or heavy shadow.** Every published elevation is black at 2–28%. `observed`: the published elevation values (private notes).
6. **Never the brand coral for an error.** Errors are burnt red `#c13515`. `observed`: the published error palette.
7. **Never a coloured header.** The header is white with one hairline under it. `observed`: on every studied screen.
8. **Never uppercase for headings or buttons.** Only the 10px captions inside the booking panel's fields are uppercase. `observed`: 3.5% of studied headings and button labels are uppercase (`words.case`); which ones is not broken down by the probe, so this is a question in [questions.md](./questions.md).
9. **Never a spinner over a page or a grid.** Loading is skeleton blocks at the size of what's coming. `inferred`: the published loading grey is a block colour, and no spinner over content was seen on any studied screen.
