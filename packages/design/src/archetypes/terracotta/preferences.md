# Preferences

What people show they want as they work with Terracotta, newest first. Record one entry whenever a person corrects a screen, picks between versions, or accepts something a check flagged. Never rewrite an entry; when a preference conflicts with the library or an earlier preference, keep both, mark them, and the newer one from the person closest to the design wins until someone settles it.

## Format

```md
### YYYY-MM-DD: <what changed, in one line>

- **Who:** <name and role>
- **Screen:** <recipe id, or the project's screen>
- **What changed:** <before → after>
- **Reach:** this screen | this kind of screen | everywhere   (if unsure, the narrowest, and ask)
- **Evidence:** told (quote or paraphrase, with where it was said)
- **Conflicts with:** <the library entry or earlier preference, or "none">
```
