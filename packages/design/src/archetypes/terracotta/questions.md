# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **Which panels lift, and why?** On the studied screens separated panels are mostly shadow-only (73%) or hairline-only (22%), and only the booking panel has both. Is "both" reserved for the commit panel on purpose, or is it a leftover of an older card style?
2. **Is the gradient for public pages and a guest's commitment only?** It appears on the booking panel, the sign-in sheet, the help pages' panel, a city landing and the host invitation's header; the host tools in the published screenshots use ink. The library now says: the gradient on public pages and for a guest's commitment, ink inside host tools and the set-up flow (`inferred`). Is that the rule, or did the host tools simply not get the gradient yet?
3. **Section headings at 500 or 600?** The place page sets them at 500 on a wide screen and 600 on a phone; help articles use 500. The library uses 600 in the app and 500 on reading pages. Which is newer, and which should a new app screen follow?
4. **Grey or ink for the line under a title?** The landing page's line under its title is grey; the help panel's sentence and the place description are ink. When is the sentence grey?
5. **The booking captions at 600?** The only uppercase seen is the booking panel's 10px captions, drawn at 700 on a page that otherwise has no 600. The library sets them at 600 so a place page keeps three weights. Is that the better reading, or should the captions keep 700 and app section headings drop to 500?
6. **Mixed button corners:** 8px (the help panel), 12px (most buttons and the sign-in sheet), full pills (the booking action, the host invitation's buttons, chips, header icons). Is the pill the newer style that the others will follow?
7. **What do the logged-in desktop pages look like?** The account page (settings) and the host's day (dashboard) are inferred: the account from public patterns, the dashboard from the phone app's published screens. Screenshots of either on a desktop would confirm or correct them, and say whether they end with the footer.
8. **Is the empty state a block in the column or a centred page?** The no-results search state was not measured (the page never settles), so the empty-state recipe is inferred.
9. **How far should recipes go on phones?** The source's phone web drops the mark and menu for a bottom tab bar, and pins the booking action to a bottom bar; recipes can't show anything only on phones, so they keep the mark's row and let the panel drop under the content. Should the library gain a phone-only bar, or keep recipes width-neutral?
10. **Dark mode:** the dark palette is published but the website renders light. Should Terracotta lead with light only, and treat dark as the phone app's?
