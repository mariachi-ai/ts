# Never

What Solace refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source screens (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen, `told` is a decision by the person who commissioned the language. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never a second action colour.** One blue fills every primary action; nothing else fills a button except the red that confirms a destructive step and the round orange play button. `observed`: the action hue is identical on all 60 studied screen-widths (`colour.accentHue` 161.5, its whole range); the language matches on all 27 of its own.
2. **Never orange for an action or a link.** Orange is the brand: the mark, one highlighted word in a big heading, the play button, the streak dots and the current step. `observed`: on the studied public pages the orange is the logo, the illustrations, the player's play button and highlighted words; links and pills are blue.
3. **Never more blue actions than the screen can say at once.** One in an app view; on a public page, the header's pill and at most one in the opening. `observed`: 1 to 2 primary actions in the first window of every studied screen (`emphasis.primaries` [1, 2]); the language keeps [1, 2].
4. **Never blue for "chosen".** A chosen plan is sun yellow, a chosen guide or period is a charcoal pill, a chosen segment is white on the warm track. `observed`: the plans page (the chosen plan filled yellow) and the published app screenshots (the chosen teacher pill charcoal).
5. **Never a square, outlined or light-weight primary.** Every button is a bold pill. `observed`: the blue pills on every studied page are fully round, and the sign-in and plans pages measure 22 to 24px control corners. On pages where the header's square menu buttons outnumber the pills the probe reads 0, so the source's `shape.controlRadius` varies [0, 24]; the language keeps [20, 24].
6. **Never a fourth weight.** Pages use two or three: 400, 700, and 500 only for small labels. `observed`: 2 or 3 weights on every studied source screen (`type.weights` [2, 3]); 2 on the plans, sign-in and not-found pages.
7. **Never a cool grey or tinted-blue quiet area in light mode.** Grouped areas, the player, the byline, empty and error bands are the warm off-white. `observed`: the warm off-white player, byline box and not-found band; the ground is white (`colour.ground` 1).
8. **Never a hairline card or a heavy shadow.** Cards take the soft 2px warm border; shadows are charcoal at 8 to 20%, and most panels are set apart by a tint, not a line. `observed`: the published shadow values (private notes); 68% of the source's separated panels are tinted, 5% bordered (`structure.separation`).
9. **Never a coloured header or a rule under it.** The header is white; colour starts below it. `observed`: on every studied page. (A promotional band above the header, in sun yellow, is seen on many public pages; the language does not carry promotions.)
10. **Never drawn people, faces or the source's artwork.** Flat token shapes stand in for every picture: discs, hills, rounded bars. `told`: the commission (copy the system, never the screen).
11. **Never uppercase for headings or buttons.** Only the settings rows' small capital actions ("EDIT", "MANAGE"). `observed`: sentence case on the studied pages; the one exception is the not-found page's older template (uppercase spaced links and button), a question in [questions.md](./questions.md).
12. **Never red for a missed day or an empty state.** Progress counts what was done; empty places are warm off-white. `inferred` from the app screenshots (empty rings for days and sessions not done).
