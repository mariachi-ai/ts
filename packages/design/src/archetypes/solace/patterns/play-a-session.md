# Play a session

**The job:** start, pause and follow something to listen to or watch.

## What Solace does

1. **The player panel:** warm off-white (`bg.sunken`), 12px corners, 16px inside, no border.
2. **A round orange play button** on the left (the brand orange with a filled play glyph), 48px.
3. Beside it, the title in bold and one muted line (the kind or the guide).
4. **A thin scrubber:** the played part in orange on the soft warm border, with the two times under it at 12px, tabular.

## Where it appears

- `dashboard` (the side column, under the streak), `list-detail` (the course's current session), `reading` (a session to try at the end of an article).
- Source: `observed` on the public meditation and sleep pages (a warm off-white player with a round orange play button, a thin track and the times at each end) and in the published app screenshots.

## Variations and triggers

- **Nothing played yet:** the scrubber is empty and the times read 0:00 and the length.
- **Night content** keeps the same player; only the art beside the title changes.
- **In a list of sessions**, each row carries its state as a circle instead (a green check, an orange play, a locked grey circle); the player is for the one session that is open.

## What it does not do

- The play button is never blue and never a pill with a label: it is the one round orange control.
- No border, shadow or white card around the player; it sits on the warm off-white.
- No volume, speed or chapter controls in the panel.

## Why

- `inferred`: the play button is the product's signature gesture, so it takes the brand colour, and the blue stays free for the one step forward on the page.
