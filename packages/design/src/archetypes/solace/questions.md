# Questions

At most ten, about reasons and conflicts, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **What does the signed-in web app look like?** Every app recipe (dashboard, library, settings, empty state) translates the phone app's published screenshots to a desktop width under the public site's header. Desktop screenshots of the signed-in product would confirm or correct the header, the side column and the widths.
2. **Does the app keep one blue pill in every view?** On the public pages a blue pill is always in the first window, often the header's offer. The language carries that into the app (Begin on the dashboard, Switch to yearly in settings). Is that the app's habit too, or only the site's, which is selling?
3. **Are pictures the norm on app screens?** The public pages lead with illustrations (media in 92% of first windows, about 8% of the window). The language draws flat token shapes as inline vectors, which the probe can't see as pictures. Should picture slots be marked as media so the measures agree?
4. **Uppercase or sentence case?** The not-found page uses uppercase spaced links and a spaced uppercase button; every other studied page is sentence case. Is the not-found page an older template?
5. **Text links: blue or charcoal?** Article bylines and inline links are blue; the plans page's legal links are orange; footer links are charcoal. Which is the rule for a new link?
6. **Two weights or three?** The plans, sign-in and not-found pages use two (400 and 700); the marketing and article pages three. Is 500 reserved for something specific?
7. **Icon size.** The public pages measure 16px icons; the app screenshots show bigger icons in the tab bar and header. Is 20px right for the app header and buttons?
8. **The sign-in sky.** The sign-in sheet floats on a photographic sky; the language uses a soft blue gradient from its tokens. Is a plain colour closer to the intent?
9. **Warm off-white vs sun yellow bands.** Public pages open on sun yellow or orange bands; the language keeps every app ground white and uses the warm off-white for bands. Should a public page open on a sun-yellow band?
10. **Dark mode.** The midnight palette is published for the app; no public page renders dark. Is dark the app's default at night, or a choice in settings?
