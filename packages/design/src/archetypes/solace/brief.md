# Solace: the brief

Warm and soothing: charcoal on white and a warm off-white, a sunny orange brand, one bright blue pill for the next step, bold rounded type and a midnight night mode. Read this first; the depth is in [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md), [patterns/](./patterns/) and [never.md](./never.md). Numbers are from the fingerprint (`fingerprint.json`); `observed` = measured or seen on the source, `inferred` = a reading of it, `told` = the commission.

## Signature moves

1. **One bright blue pill, always in view.** It names the next step: the header's offer on a public page, Begin on the day's card, Continue at the foot of a flow. `observed`: one action hue on every studied source screen; 1–2 primary actions in every first window.
2. **Orange is the brand, never a button:** the mark's tile, one word of a big heading, the round play button, the streak dots, the current step. `observed`.
3. **The warm off-white holds things:** the player, the byline, choice rows, the membership panel, empty and error bands, quiet pills, the footer. `observed`.
4. **Bold, round, tracked in:** 700 headings and pill labels at −3%; every button a pill with a 2px press-shadow. `observed`.
5. **Time as a line of dots:** done is a green tick, now is orange, later is an empty ring (the day, the week, a course, a trial). `observed` in the app's published screenshots.
6. **Flat token shapes** stand in for every picture; no faces. `told`.

## Refusals

No second action colour · no orange button or link · no blue for "chosen" (sun yellow or charcoal) · no square or outlined primary · no fourth weight · no cool grey quiet areas · no hairline cards or heavy shadows · no coloured header · no drawn people · no uppercase but the settings row actions · no red for what wasn't done. Evidence in [never.md](./never.md).

## Two families

The **app** (dashboard, library, settings, empty, errors) uses the 80px app header and one blue pill per view. **Flows and sign-in** drop the header for a step count or the mark alone over a soft blue sky. **Public pages** (pricing, reading) use the site header with "Log in" and its blue pill, 48px bold titles and 18px text, and end with the warm off-white footer. See DESIGN.md, Page shapes.

## The five habits that matter most

1. **The same blue, the same place in the order.** `observed`: action hue 161.5 on all 60 source screen-widths and all 27 of the language's; primary actions per first window [1, 2] on both.
2. **Few sizes, few weights.** Four to six text sizes, two or three weights (500 only for small labels). `observed`: source sizes [3, 6] and weights [2, 3]; the language [4, 6] and [2, 3].
3. **Tint before line.** Panels are set apart by the warm off-white, then by 2px soft rules; a border is for cards, a shadow for the one raised card or a sheet. `observed`: of the source's separated panels 68% are tinted, 22% divided, 5% bordered; the language 80%, 17%, 5%.
4. **Round controls.** Pills and 12px fields. `observed`: control corners 20–24px in the language; 22–24px on the source's sign-in and plans pages (the source's median reads lower where square menu buttons dominate).
5. **Big, bold titles over 16–18px text.** `observed`: the largest text is 1.7–3× the body in the language, 1.7–4.4× on the source (its public pages run bigger; the app screens are `inferred` nearer 2×).
