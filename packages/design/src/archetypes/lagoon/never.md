# Never

What Lagoon refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites what was seen in the
source's public help screenshots of its own window (ids in the private notes) or a measured number,
`inferred` is a reading of what was seen, `told` is a rule someone set. The recipes follow every one.
Numbers are from the fingerprint (`fingerprint.json`, 27 screen-widths).

1. **Never a header bar with a rule across the thread.** Controls float as round glass buttons at the
   pane's top corners; the person's circle sits over a name capsule. `observed`: the source's window
   in every public help screenshot of it has no toolbar over the conversation.
2. **Never a second colour for your bubbles, and never a tint on theirs.** Yours are the one blue,
   theirs a flat light grey. `observed` (blue and grey bubbles in the help screenshots); `told` for
   leaving out the source's second bubble colour for another service (one service only).
3. **Never a time inside a bubble, and never a receipt under every message.** Timestamps sit between
   runs, centred; one receipt sits under your last message. `observed` in the help screenshots.
4. **Never a tail on every bubble.** Only the last of a run. `observed` in the help screenshots.
5. **Never blur, frost or translucency.** Glass is drawn opaque with a hairline ring and a soft drop.
   `told`: a deliberate difference from the source, recorded in the private notes.
6. **Never a square button or a square field.** Buttons, search, segments, badges and the composer are
   capsules; icon buttons and avatars are circles. `observed`: capsules and circles throughout the
   help screenshots.
7. **Never a border round a card or a panel.** Grouped cards are white on the grouped grey with 20px
   corners and a faint ring; separators are hairlines inset to the text. `observed` on the phone's
   settings and the desktop's sheets in the help screenshots. The recipes' separation is tint on
   0.90 of separated containers, a border on 0.03.
8. **Never photos, characters, illustrations or app artwork.** People are initials on soft tints;
   empty and error states use a large grey outline glyph. `told`: the language copies no assets.
9. **Never more than one blue capsule to press in the window or a sheet.** The current row or pin is
   the only other solid blue. A public page allows the site bar's capsule plus one. `inferred` from
   the source's screens. Measured on the recipes: one primary in the window on 20 of 27
   screen-widths, none on 3 phone widths, two on pricing's widest (site bar and the suggested plan);
   settings reads 3 only because, with no blue capsule on it, the probe takes its green switches as
   the accent.
10. **Never large feature glyphs.** Icons are 18px in round buttons, 15–16px beside text, and only
    empty and error states go large. `observed` on the recipes: icons.size 18 [15.3, 18] over 24
    screen-widths; `inferred` from the source's own setup sheet, which uses round tinted buttons,
    not a list of features.
