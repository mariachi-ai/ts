# Questions

The questions whose answers would change Lagoon the most, about reasons and conflicts. Each says what
is known now and what would change.

1. **Is the app's own dark mode what the language draws?** No current dark screenshot of the source's
   window was found; dark is built from the platform's published dark colours. A real one would
   check the thread, their bubbles and the sidebar first.
2. **Should switches be green or blue?** The phone's switches are green; the desktop's own settings
   draw them in the accent. The window is the desktop's, the switches are the phone's. If blue,
   the switch tokens change and settings' accent becomes blue.
3. **How big is a public page's headline?** The source's public pages run a 3–6× headline over 17px
   text; the app's largest title is about twice its text. Pricing sits at 3.7× and reading at 2×.
   Should public pages keep their larger scale, or come down toward the app's?
4. **Should buttons be medium (500) or regular?** The desktop's buttons read regular in the help
   screenshots; the language uses 500, which makes four weights (400, 500, 600, 700) on most
   screens. If regular, the language drops to three.
5. **What does a first run look like?** `onboarding` follows the source's name-and-picture sheet,
   seen in a help screenshot; the source's sign-in was held out unseen. Is a separate welcome with
   features ever shown?
6. **Where does a banner go on the web?** Lagoon puts it at the window's top right, as the desktop's
   notifications arrive. Inside a web app, should it sit in the thread instead?
7. **Is the bubble gradient wanted?** The source paints your bubbles as a light-to-system-blue
   gradient down the window; Lagoon draws one flat, deeper blue so white text holds 4.5:1.
8. **Do settings tiles keep 15px glyphs?** They are the one place icons drop below 16px; the
   desktop's own tiles look smaller than the phone's.
