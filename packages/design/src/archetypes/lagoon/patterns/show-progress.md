# Show progress

**The job:** say that something is happening or how far it has got.

## What Lagoon does

- **Someone is writing:** three grey dots in a grey bubble with a tail, where their next message will appear.
- **A message's journey:** "**Delivered**", then "**Read** 9:42 AM", in 11px grey under your last message; nothing on earlier ones.
- **A step of setting up:** "Step 1 of 2" in grey at the top right of the column.
- **Loading:** round avatar skeletons with two grey bars for rows; a button keeps its width and swaps its label for a small grey spinner.
- **Something measurable** (storage, an upload): a thin 4px capsule bar, blue on a light grey track.

## Where it appears

- `dashboard` (typing, Delivered), `reading` (Read), `onboarding` (the step).

## Variations and triggers

- **Near a limit:** the bar turns orange, then red.

## What it does not do

- No percentages on messages; no progress bar for sending; no spinner over the thread.
- No receipt under every message.

## Why

- `inferred`: the reference shows one receipt under your last message and a typing bubble, read from its public help screenshots; the rest keeps that quietness.
