# Move between areas

**The job:** get from one area to another and know where you are.

## What Lagoon does

- **In the window:** the sidebar is the navigation. Its top holds the wordmark and two round glass buttons (filter, new chat); a segmented control for the product's sections; then circles and rows. The current place is the solid blue row or pin.
- **Inside a page:** round glass back and forward buttons beside the title; a round close button for a sheet.
- **On a phone:** the list and the page are two screens; the page reached from the list comes first.
- **On public pages:** the site bar: the wordmark, four grey links (the current one in a grey capsule), a blue text "Sign in" and one blue capsule; a small grey footer of links.

## Where it appears

- Every window recipe (`dashboard`, `list-detail`, `settings`, `empty-state`, `errors`); `pricing` and `reading` (the site bar); `reading` (a breadcrumb with "›").

## Variations and triggers

- **Settings:** the sidebar's rows lead with coloured tiles instead of circles.
- **A help article:** a breadcrumb above the title.

## What it does not do

- No rail of icons, no top navigation bar in the app, no hamburger on desktop.
- No breadcrumbs inside the app; back and forward buttons instead.
- No second sidebar.

## Why

- `inferred`: the reference's window has only the sidebar and floating round buttons; there is no app bar to put navigation in.
