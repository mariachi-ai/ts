# Choose an option

**The job:** pick one of a few options (a view, a period, a page style, an accent, a theme).

## What Quire does

1. **A segmented capsule** for views and periods: a white capsule on the faint ring; the chosen segment a grey wash; icons (cards, list, board) or one or two words (Mine, Everyone; Yearly, Monthly).
2. **Tiles** for looks: grey tiles with 10px corners and a sample ("Ag" in each face, a tiny window for a theme); the chosen one ringed in 2px blue, its name in blue.
3. **Swatches** for colour: 24px circles; the chosen one ringed with a white gap and a grey ring.
4. **A small select** in a settings row, sized to its text.

## Where it appears

- `dashboard` and `list-detail` (segmented views), `pricing` (Yearly/Monthly), `onboarding` (page-style tiles, accent swatches), `settings` (theme tiles, swatches, a select).

## Variations and triggers

- **Two to four options** with short names or icons → segmented; **looks** → tiles; **colours** → swatches; **more than five** → a select.

## What it does not do

- No radio-button lists for looks; no dropdown for two options.
- No blue fill on a chosen segment: it is a grey wash.

## Why

- `observed`: the source's pricing page uses a white segmented capsule for Yearly/Monthly with the chosen segment raised; the app's view switch and style pickers are from the screenshots in the private notes (`inferred`).
