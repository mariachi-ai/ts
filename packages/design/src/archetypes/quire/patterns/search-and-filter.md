# Search and filter

**The job:** narrow a list to what someone wants.

## What Quire does

1. **A search capsule** at the toolbar's right: a white capsule with a 16px search icon and a 12px chevron.
2. **In a sidebar,** a small search field on the grey.
3. **Filters as a segmented capsule** (Mine, Everyone) beside a round sort button.

## Where it appears

- `dashboard` and `list-detail` (search capsule, segments, sort), `reading` (the search field in the contents), `empty-state` (the toolbar keeps its round buttons).

## Variations and triggers

- **A list** → the capsule and segments; **a page's contents** → the field in the sidebar.

## What it does not do

- No filter chips row above the list; no filter sidebar.
- No search field that spans the toolbar.

## Why

- `observed`: the search field at the top of the source's shared-document sidebar. `inferred`: the toolbar's search capsule and segments from the app screenshots in the private notes.
