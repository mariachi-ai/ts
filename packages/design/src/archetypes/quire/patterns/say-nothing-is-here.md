# Say nothing is here

**The job:** say that a view is empty, why, and what to do.

## What Quire does

1. **The frame stays:** the sidebar and the toolbar (round add, the title, round buttons).
2. **Centred in the main area,** at most 26rem wide: a 44px thin outline icon in subtle grey, a 19px semibold heading, one muted sentence that says what will appear here and how.
3. **Two capsules:** the one blue action ("Schedule a draft") and a white one ("Open drafts").

## Where it appears

- `empty-state` (Scheduled).

## What it does not do

- No illustrations, no characters, no emoji.
- No card or sheet round an empty state.
- No more than one blue capsule.

## Why

- `inferred`: built from the library, not a screen (the private notes record that the source's empty states were not public); it keeps the app's title size and one blue action.
