# Edit a setting

**The job:** let someone see a value and change it, often at once.

## What Quire does

1. **A settings column** (max 46rem) beside a settings sidebar: the space's 64px tile and name on top, then groups of rows.
2. **The title row:** a round close button, a round back button, the section's title at 19px semibold.
3. **Sections:** a 13px semibold title and, where it helps, one muted 13px line; then the rows.
4. **Grouped rows** on grey (`sunken`), 12px corners, 16px side padding, hairlines between rows: the setting in 13px at the left (with what it does in muted 12px under it, where it needs saying), the control at the right: a system switch, a small select, or a blue text action ("Edit").
5. **Switches apply at once;** there is no Save button.

## Where it appears

- `settings` (Appearance), `onboarding` (a name field and pickers), `errors` (the domain form).

## Variations and triggers

- **A value with a few looks** → tiles in a grey group (see [choose-an-option](./choose-an-option.md)).
- **A form that changes something outside** (a domain) → a sheet with fields, Cancel and one blue capsule.

## What it does not do

- No white cards for settings groups: they are grey.
- No Save bar for switches.
- No labels above switches: the label is at the left of its row.

## Why

- `inferred` from the source's settings screenshots in the private notes (grey grouped rows `#eeefef` on `#f5f6f6`, switches and blue text actions); the 12px hint follows the language's small-label size.
