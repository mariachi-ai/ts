# Never

What Quire refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites what was measured or
seen on the source's public pages and shared documents (ids in the private notes), `inferred` is a
reading of what was seen, `told` is a rule someone set. The recipes follow every one. Numbers are from
the fingerprint (`fingerprint.json`, 27 screen-widths).

1. **Never document text at interface size.** A page's text is 16px on a 24px line; the chrome is 13px.
   `observed`: the source's shared documents set 16px body under 13px sidebar rows. Recipes: `list-detail`,
   `reading`.
2. **Never a title more than about twice the text inside the app.** The largest text is 1.5 [1.46, 2.06]
   times the body: 19 over 13 in the window, 24 over 16 on a page, 24 over 13 on a form. Only the public
   pricing page goes higher (2.4–3.4). `observed`: the source's shared documents 1.41–1.85; its marketing
   pages 2.5–4.1.
3. **Never a border round a sheet, a card or a block.** Sheets and cards float on soft shadows; blocks are
   tints; only tables draw hairlines. `observed`: the source's shared documents (white sheets on a
   shadow, borderless blocks). Recipes: every one.
4. **Never a square button.** Buttons, segments and search are capsules, icon buttons circles; rows and
   fields have 10px corners. Control corners 10 [10, 16]. `observed`: capsules on the source's public
   pages (16 [1, 22]); `inferred` for the app.
5. **Never a coloured bar or a blue fill for the current row.** It is a white tile with the capsule ring on
   the grey sidebar. `inferred` from the app screenshots in the private notes; the shared documents'
   contents mark it with a grey wash.
6. **Never more than one blue action in a view.** Other actions are white capsules or ghosts; public pages
   use an ink capsule instead of blue. Measured: at most one primary in the window on every recipe except
   `settings`, where the probe counts the blue switches. `inferred` from the source's screens.
7. **Never photos, illustrations, logos or emoji in the system.** Covers and backdrops are token gradients;
   folder icons are outlines in tint colours. `told`: the language copies no assets (private notes).
8. **Never a coloured stripe or border on a tinted block.** `observed`: the source's block decorations are
   flat tints with 6px corners.
9. **Never uppercase in the interface.** Group names, column names and buttons are sentence case; capitals
   only for the public pages' small kickers and plan names. `observed` on the source's shared documents and pricing page.
10. **Never a person as initials in a table or a byline.** A person in a page is a mention on the blue
    mention highlight. `observed`: a mention in a table cell in the source's shared documents.
