# Questions

The questions whose answers would change Quire the most, about reasons and conflicts. Each says what is
known now and what would change.

1. **Is the app's window grey or white?** The language's window is a pale grey (`#f8f8f8`) from the
   desktop app's screenshots; every public page measured is white or warm paper (ground 0.98–1). If the
   web app is white, the canvas token and every app recipe's ground change.
2. **What sits behind a published page?** The source floats its sheets on a photo or a colour backdrop;
   the language uses a soft token gradient. Should a published page ever sit on plain grey instead?
3. **Do settings ever explain a row?** The 12px hint under a switch row is the language's small-label
   size, not seen on a measured settings screen. If settings rows never carry a hint, settings drops to two
   text sizes.
4. **How do first-use pages look?** Onboarding and sign-in are built from the library (the web sign-in
   page was behind a login wall and not measured). Are they white sheets over a cover, or full-window?
5. **Does a page ever lead with a cover?** The source's shared documents often open with a large cover
   image above the title; the language's pages open with the title, and covers live only on cards.
6. **How large are icons in the app?** The source's shared documents draw icons in 20px boxes (16px
   glyphs) and 24px footer buttons; the language uses 16px everywhere but chips. If the app's are 20, the
   icon check moves.
7. **Are tables of people mentions or avatars?** The language draws a person as a mention, as the source's
   documents do in tables; the app's collection views may show avatars.
8. **Is the public pages' serif ever used in the app?** It appears only in the serif page style and the
   public pages; onboarding now uses the sans page title.
9. **Should the error page follow the public 404?** The source's public 404 is a marketing page with a large
   serif headline; the language's whole-page error is an app sheet with a 19px title.
10. **Which accent colours are offered?** The source lets people choose an accent; the language ships the
    default blue only.
