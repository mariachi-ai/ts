# Preferences

What people show they want as they work, newest first. Record one entry whenever a person corrects a screen, picks between versions, or accepts something a check flagged. A preference that conflicts with the library keeps both entries, marked; the newer one from the person closest to the design wins until someone settles it.

Format:

```md
## YYYY-MM-DD: <what changed, in a few words>
- **Who:** <name and role>
- **Screen:** <recipe id or project screen>
- **Before → after:** <what it was, what they wanted>
- **Reach:** this screen | this kind of screen | everywhere (if unsure, the narrowest, and ask)
- **Conflicts with:** <library entry, or "none">
- **Evidence:** told (<where they said it>)
```
