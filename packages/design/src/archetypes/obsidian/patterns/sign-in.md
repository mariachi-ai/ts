# Sign in

**Job.** Let someone get into the product in one choice, with the method they already use first.

## What Obsidian does (parts in order)

1. The frame colour (the darkest step) in the person's own theme, light or dark; nothing else on the page.
2. The product's mark at 48px, monochrome in the default text colour.
3. A 17px/510 title, 24px above the first button.
4. **An 18rem column of 44px pills**, 16px apart, full column width: the first method is the one primary, solid indigo; the others are secondary grey pills. Text only, 13px/510.
5. One footnote line in 13px muted, 32px below, with default-text links that aren't underlined: create an account or learn more (sign-up adds the terms).

## Where it appears

- `sign-in`.
- Source study: the sign-in and sign-up pages, in light and dark, at three widths (both have the same column: 288px of 44px pills, the first in indigo; no inputs on the first screen).

## Variations and triggers

- **Sign-up:** the same column with a different title and three methods; the footnote carries the terms and a link back to sign-in.
- **The email step:** one 44px email field with 8px corners and its label above, then a 44px indigo pill to continue (`inferred`: the step isn't public; see `questions.md`).
- **Phone:** the same 18rem column, centred.

## What it does not do

- No navbar, footer, illustration, product picture or split screen.
- No provider logos or brand colours on third-party buttons; no "or" divider.
- No 6px app buttons, no inverted site pills: sign-in takes indigo from the app and the pill from the site.
- No required-field asterisk.

## Why

- `observed`: see "Where it appears" (measured: 288 × 44px pills with a full radius, the first `#5e69d1`, 13px/500 labels; title 18px/500; footnote 13px muted; page `#09090a` in dark).
- `inferred`: sign-in is the door to the app, so it already wears the person's theme and the app's indigo; the pills keep it continuous with the site they came from.
