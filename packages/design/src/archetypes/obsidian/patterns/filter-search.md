# Filter and search

**Job.** Narrow a long list, or jump to anything by name.

## What Obsidian does (parts in order)

- **Filter bar** under the header bar: a ghost **Filter** (list-filter icon) on the left, a secondary **Display** on the right. Applied filters appear as chips in this bar (seen in public screenshots; not a recipe).
- **Display** opens a menu of grouping, ordering, toggles and the properties to show as small chips.
- **Search and everything else** go through the command menu (`⌘K`) or the sidebar's search icon: one input, results as menu rows.
- **Public pages** (`pricing`-style): a pill search field with a `⌘K` key hint at the right (source study: the integrations and writing index pages).

## Where it appears

- `list-detail`: the Filter / Display bar.
- `onboarding`: the command menu as the way to find and do things.
- Sidebar search button in every app recipe.

## Variations and triggers

- One list → the filter bar. Anything across the product → the command menu.
- No filter applied → only the Filter button; nothing else takes room.

## What it does not do

- No always-open filter sidebar or facets column.
- No search field in the app's header bar; no results page.
- No "Apply" button: filters apply as they are chosen.

## Why

- `inferred`: filters are occasional, lists are constant; the bar keeps one quiet row for them.
- `observed`: the command menu is the product's main input (recipe `onboarding` teaches it as the first lesson).
