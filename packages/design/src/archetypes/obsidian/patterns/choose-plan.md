# Choose a plan (public pages)

**Job.** Compare a few plans and start the right one.

## What Obsidian does (parts in order)

1. The 72px translucent navbar.
2. A 48px display title alone, then straight into the plans.
3. **Plan columns split by hairlines**, no cards: name 24px at 510, price 17px with a muted unit, a billing line between two hairlines (a switch for yearly), ticked features, then a 40px pill button at the bottom; the one to pick is inverted (light on dark).
4. A comparison table: 12px muted header, 15px muted feature names, ticks and crosses.
5. Questions as hairline accordion rows.

## Where it appears

- `pricing`.
- Source study: the public pricing page (four columns split by hairlines; separation divider 1.0, border 0, shadow 0 at all three widths).

## Variations and triggers

- Four plans in a row on wide; two by two on narrow; stacked on phone.

## What it does not do

- No cards, shadows or "most popular" ribbons; no coloured plan headers.
- No glow, gradient or illustration behind the title (removed in the faithfulness pass: the source page has none).
- No indigo buttons on public pages: the main action is inverted, the rest translucent grey.

## Why

- `observed`: see "Where it appears".
- `inferred`: on public pages the inverted pill does the job the indigo button does in the app.
