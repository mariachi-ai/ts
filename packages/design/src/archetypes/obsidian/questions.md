# Questions

At most ten, ordered by how much the answer would change the library.

1. **Onboarding: the first-use pages' type and the app's button, or the first-use pages throughout?** Onboarding (behind sign-up, not seen) now takes the type of the public sign-up and log-in pages (a 17px/510 title over 13px, two weights) but keeps the app's 6px indigo Continue at 20rem, while sign-in uses 44px pills. Does setup continue sign-in's pills, and is the sign-in email field a pill or a rectangle?
2. **Settings rows: why so tall?** Settings rows measure 66.5px (two lines and 14px padding) where every other list in the language sits at 27.5–44px. The source's settings were seen only in public screenshots. Are the rows really that tall, or does the product keep settings at the sidebar's 28–32px with the help text elsewhere?
3. **Icons: 14px in the app, 16px on public pages. One rule or two?** In the source's HTML app frame sidebar rows and header buttons carry 14px icons and priority bars 16px; its docs and plans pages carry 16px. Is the split by family on purpose, or does the app use 16px in places the public frame didn't show?
4. **Cards inside the app: when, if ever?** Only comments and settings panels are hairline boxes; charts, figures and lists sit on the panel. Is there a screen where the product does use cards in the app (a board view?) and what are its rules?
5. **Why does the site invert the main action while the app and sign-in use indigo?** Recorded, not explained: the site is always dark and never indigo; sign-in follows the person's theme and is. Is indigo reserved for inside the product on purpose, with sign-in counted as inside?
6. **Headings in the app: are there any above 24px?** The app recipes top out at 24px (heading ratio 1.85); public pages go to 48–64px. Is there an app screen (a document, a project overview) with a display-size title?
7. **The light theme: equal twin or secondary?** The language is measured in dark, as studied; light keeps a 1px contact shadow on cards. Is light used enough that it should get its own measured source?
8. **Grouping bars versus header rows.** Lists group under grey bars with no header row; the projects list has a header row. What decides it: the number of columns, or the kind of item?
9. **Quaternary text.** The source's faintest text fails 4.5:1, so the language uses a brighter grey. Is the faint step important enough to keep for non-essential text (timestamps) at 3:1?
10. **Phone.** The phone layout stacks the sidebar above the panel because the native phone app wasn't studied. What does the phone app put first?
