# Show one thing

**The job:** show one session, route or segment in full, with its figures, its map and how it compares.

## What Summit does

1. **A side column of sections** (11rem, a hairline frame): Summary, Splits, Segments with a count, then a 12px grey capital heading over the members' sections; the current one near-black and semibold with a 3px orange bar.
2. **A header card:** a grey title bar ("Name – Ride" with the props and comment counts), then the avatar, date, 32px title, one grey line and tags beside [the figures](./show-the-numbers.md) in two rows.
3. **The map and the elevation profile** in one card, the selected stretch pale orange with an orange top line.
4. **A table of segments**, the open one marked by the orange bar, its detail below: this effort against the previous best, white buttons for what to do next, and the top ten.

## Where it appears

- `list-detail`; `reading` is the public, long-form one-thing page.
- Source: `observed` on the public route pages (title, one orange button to use the route, figures under hairlines, the map beside it, the elevation profile below); the side column, header card and segments `observed` in the published screenshots of session pages.

## Variations and triggers

- **A route** (no effort yet): the title, one orange button to use it, the figures and the map side by side.
- **An article** (`reading`): breadcrumb, 32px title, a 16px grey lede, the byline, 20px section headings, the sections in a ruled column at the right.

## What it does not do

- No orange button for the secondary actions (Compare, Set a target): they are white.
- No tabs across the top of the page for sections; the side column holds them.
- Never more than two rows of figures in the header.

## Why

- `inferred`: one session is studied, not skimmed; the side column lets someone move through its parts while the header keeps the result in view.
