# Say nothing is here

**The job:** say a place is empty and what to do about it.

## What Summit does

1. The frame stays: the bar, the page title with its white button, the tabs.
2. **One white card**, centred text, 48px padding: a token-drawn dashed route on the quiet fill (start dot green, end ring dark), a 24px title at 600 ("No saved routes yet"), one 16px grey sentence, then an orange and a white button.

## Where it appears

- `empty-state`.
- `inferred`: no empty screen is public; built from the source's card, button and map treatment.

## Variations and triggers

- **Empty because of a filter:** the same card says which filter and offers "Clear filters" as a white button.
- **Inside a side card:** one 12px grey line, no picture.

## What it does not do

- No illustration of people, no mascot, no photo.
- No second orange button; the title's button is white.

## Why

- `inferred`: an empty place is an invitation to go out and record; the dashed route says "not yet" in the product's own picture.
