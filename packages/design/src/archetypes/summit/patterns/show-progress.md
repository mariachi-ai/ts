# Show progress

**The job:** show how far along something is: a challenge, a weekly goal, a flow, a week of sessions.

## What Summit does

- **A thin rounded bar** on the hairline grey, filled with the action orange (light mode) or the signature orange (dark), the figure in 12px under or beside it ("62.3 of 100 km · 18 days left").
- **The week** as seven small bars: days with a session in the signature orange, rest days as a 2px grey stub, single 12px day letters, no axis.
- **A flow** shows "STEP 2 OF 4" in 12px grey capitals over the same thin bar.

## Where it appears

- `dashboard` (challenges, the week, the weekly goal), `onboarding` (steps).
- Source: `observed`: the challenges page (a badge, the goal and dates, one orange join button); the week bars and goal `observed` in the published screenshots of the web dashboard; the plans page shows a trial as a vertical timeline (today, a reminder, the charge).

## Variations and triggers

- **Over the goal:** the bar stays full; the figure says by how much.
- **A meter** (a limit): green with room, yellow near it, red over it.

## What it does not do

- No percentages without the real figure; no rings or dials.
- No red for a missed day or week.

## Why

- `inferred`: progress is a figure first; the bar only shows its share.
