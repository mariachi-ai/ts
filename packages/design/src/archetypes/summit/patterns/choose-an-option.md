# Choose an option

**The job:** pick one or several of a few options: sports, a plan, a period, whose sessions to show.

## What Summit does

- **Sports** are round tiles (64px circles on the quiet fill, a 20px icon, the name under it in 14px); picked ones fill pale orange with a 2px orange ring and an orange name.
- **Plans** are cards with 16px corners on the dark band; the picked one is light with the orange button, the others dark with white outlined buttons.
- **A period** (Yearly or Monthly) is the segmented control: a grey track with 8px corners and a light knob.
- **A filter** is a small select (32px) above a feed or board, or a row of 4px outlined chips.

## Where it appears

- `onboarding` (sports), `pricing` (plans, the period), `dashboard` (whose sessions), `list-detail` (the board's period).
- Source: `observed`: the plans page's cards with large corners, the challenges page's outlined chips with icons, the public pages' selects; the sport choice `observed` in the published screenshots of the phone app.

## Variations and triggers

- **More than six options:** a select, not tiles.
- **One plan only:** no cards; a single offer and its button.

## What it does not do

- No radio lists for choices that apply at once; no switches in settings (see [edit-a-setting](./edit-a-setting.md)).
- No orange fill for a picked option other than the pale orange tile; the orange button stays the action.
- No more than three plan cards.

## Why

- `inferred`: choices are quick and visual; the orange is saved for the action that follows.
