# Never

What Summit refuses to do. Each refusal carries how it is known: `observed` cites the source's screens (ids in the private notes) or a measured number, `inferred` is a reading of what was seen, `told` is a decision by the person who commissioned the language. The recipes follow every one.

1. **Never a third weight.** Text is 400 and everything that stands out is 600: titles, names, labels, buttons, prices. No 500, 700 or 800. `observed`: 2 weights on most of the source's 20 studied public pages (`type.weights` typical 2, range [2, 3]); the log-in, route and page-not-found pages set their titles and buttons at 600. The language keeps [2, 2] on all nine recipes.
2. **Never a size off the ramp, and never more than five on a screen.** 12, 14, 16, 20, 24 and 32px; units are 14px or 12px, not a share of the figure. `observed`: the source's screens use about four sizes (`type.sizes` typical 4, range [2.9, 5]); the language [4, 5].
3. **Never a headline more than about twice the body.** Page titles, plans headlines and the 404 are 32px over 14–16px text. `observed`: `type.headingRatio` typical 2 on the source (the route, log-in and help pages 1.75 to 2.3); the language [1.9, 2.3]. Bigger headlines appear only over photographs on the source's marketing pages, which the language does not draw.
4. **Never three orange buttons in one window.** The bar's offer and the page's one action at most. `observed`: 0 to 2 primary actions in every studied first window, most often the header's join button at the top right.
5. **Never the signature orange as a button fill or under small text.** It is for routes, the current-item line and bars, achievements and the wordmark; actions use the darker action orange. `observed`: the source's own hover step is the language's fill; white on the signature orange is 3.3:1, under the 4.5:1 the library requires.
6. **Never a second action hue.** Links, buttons and counts are the one orange; blue is for focus only. `observed`: the action hue is identical on every studied page that has one (`colour.accentHue` 299.3 on 43 of 60 screen-widths). The source's blue text links in tables and legal lines are not carried over (private notes).
7. **Never rounded pill buttons or soft 12–24px cards in the app.** Buttons, fields and cards are 4px; only the plan cards and their buttons take 16px. `observed`: control corners 4px on the source (`shape.controlRadius` typical 4); the plans page alone uses large corners.
8. **Never switches or radio lists in settings.** Values are rows with a label, the value in words and a pencil. `observed` in a published screenshot of the web settings page.
9. **Never a photo, a real map tile, a logo or a drawn face in a recipe.** Maps and pictures are token-drawn placeholders; avatars are initials. `told`: the commission (copy the system, never the screen).
10. **Never a grey or tinted block for the current item in the bar.** The current top-bar link has the 2px signature-orange line at the bar's foot. `observed` on the public pages and in the published web screenshots.
